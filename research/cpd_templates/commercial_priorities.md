# Commercial priorities

## Preliminary UK assessment · 19 November 2026

See the [updated ranking below](#international-programme-ranking--20-november-2026) for current recommendations.

### Method

Rank by frequency and consequences of recording, specificity of fields, flexibility of the official recording format, fit with local evidence and reporting, and strength of available sources. Use regulator registrant data as an upper-bound denominator and account for role, jurisdiction, employer tools and official alternatives before estimating a reachable audience. International reuse means portable workflow components plus local scheme rules. App users, addressable demand and willingness to pay have not been measured. Rankings are provisional product inferences.

CPD Progress Tracker’s plausible advantage is local mobile capture with original evidence and portable exports at £3.99 once per template. It needs demonstrably faster recording or a more useful summary than official alternatives. Before approval, test journeys using sample records, ask users to complete the same work in their current tools, then measure performance and ask about the actual proposed price. Price and conversion remain hypotheses.

## International programme ranking · 20 November 2026

This ranking supersedes the preliminary ranking above. The earlier section records the starting hypothesis and keeps its sources. Scores are qualitative prioritisation inputs, not sales likelihood. Each dimension uses 1 for weak, unverified or difficult, 3 for mixed and 5 for strong. The weighted total is the sum of each score divided by five and multiplied by its weight. Break ties in favour of the clearer combination of scope, geographic reuse and evidence of independent recording.

| Scoring dimension | Weight | What drives the score |
|---|---|---|
| Eligible audience | 15 | Dated register or membership denominator and a reachable subsegment. Penalise missing role-specific evidence. |
| Recording burden | 15 | Frequency, validation rules, document retention, consequences and recurring administrative friction. |
| Standalone opportunity | 25 | Independent capture accepted, clear advantage over official and competitor tools, low forced duplication. |
| Initial distribution | 15 | UK-first channel accessibility, language and portability in the same scheme, with local variants handled separately. |
| Build and maintenance viability | 20 | Fit with visual fields, data recovery and versioned reporting, transparent exceptions, support workload. High means easier to sustain. |
| Confidence | 10 | Current authoritative guidance, published example and open questions narrow enough to test. |

### Product economics and reasons to pass

The £3.99 template and £12.99 Custom Fields price are fixed programme assumptions. A buyer needing both would spend £16.98. At UK VAT of 20% and indicative store fees of 15–30% on ex-VAT value, proceeds before refunds are approximately £2.33–£2.83 per template and £7.58–£9.20 for Custom Fields. Country, account and platform conditions vary. At an assumed 5% fully refunded sales and £500 annual direct upkeep, a template would need about 187 sales at the 15% rate or 227 at 30% to cover that upkeep alone. This threshold excludes build, support escalation and user acquisition. The upkeep and refund assumptions are scenario inputs. [Competitor pricing, fee sources and sensitivity](international/competitor_comparison.md)

- **Template fields are copyable and should stay reusable.** Some Custom Fields owners will recreate simple layouts rather than pay for a preset. Sell the reviewed scheme setup, well-chosen defaults, reliable calculations where approved, and clear update and export behaviour. Keep the Field Library useful on its own and never block a similar independently built layout.
- **A free official workflow can win despite complex rules.** RCVS, RICS and IOSH require their own systems. Their compliance burden does not create strong demand for duplicating entry. Social Work England and GPhC may justify a drafting tool only if transfer time drops measurably.
- **£12.99 needs clear value for repeat users.** Test with people maintaining multiple roles, custom employer requirements or specialised evidence. A single-scheme user may need only one £3.99 template or a spreadsheet. Competing subscriptions often bundle learning, workspaces or appraisal features; their prices do not validate willingness to buy the add-on.
- **One-time sales fund continuing obligations unevenly.** Use a bounded set of maintained schemes, precise scope and dated policies. Track actual support effort, update frequency and refund reasons. Defer any scheme whose ongoing verification cost cannot be justified by observed sales and shared components. Keep historical templates usable if a scheme is retired from sale.
- **Trust is product value.** Validate that users can recover records and evidence, understand local storage and export privacy, and see who verified a template and when. Use jurisdiction and scheme filters to keep a shared Field Library approachable. Official submission status and evidence eligibility should always be user-reviewable.

### Naming, marks and published material

Use plain scheme references to identify scope, with the app name and a clear independent companion description. Avoid regulator logos, endorsement wording, copied forms or substantial guidance text unless permission or an applicable licence is confirmed. The UK Trade Marks Act section 11 includes limited referential use tied to honest practices. NMC’s terms reserve rights in its information and trademarks and describe personal or internal-use permissions. Confirm the specific design, marks, quoted choice text, forms and each launch jurisdiction before marketing. No naming clearance was established for the shortlisted products.
