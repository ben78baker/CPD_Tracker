# United States · California attorney MCLE

**Checked 20 November 2026.** State Bar of California, active attorneys in the standard minimum continuing legal education route. **Recommendation: validate first.** Specialty, exemption, proportionate hours and early-career rules may change outcomes.

## Requirements, audit and reporting

The standard cycle requires **25 credit hours** with no more than **12.5 self-study**. Subfields include **4 legal ethics**, **2 elimination of bias** (including 1 implicit bias/bias reduction), **2 competence** (including 1 prevention/detection), **1 technology**, and **1 civility**. Subfield recognition follows accepted award criteria and may be within a more general activity. Rules prohibit carrying excess into the next period for this standard route. Use assigned group and official dates rather than a generic three-year timer. At this check A–G reports by 30 March 2028, H–M by 30 March 2027, and N–Z last reported by 30 March 2026 following a transitional extended window. [State Bar Title 2 rules, rr. 2.70–2.73](https://www.calbar.ca.gov/legal-professionals/rules/rules-state-bar/title-2-rights-and-responsibilities-licensees) · [State Bar compliance groups](https://www.calbar.ca.gov/legal-professionals/maintaining-compliance/mcle/compliance-groups)

Attorneys track hours independently. The Bar offers a Personal MCLE Log. Public pages are mixed about online credit tracking. Its compliance page says the Bar does not track attorneys' education hours, while a related link says to log in to My State Bar Profile to report or track credits. Authenticated features and provider feeds remain unverified. [Bar compliance overview](https://www.calbar.ca.gov/mcle-compliance/who-must-report-mcle-compliance) Retain applicable attendance certificates, self-study records and exemption proof for **one year after reporting**. This is the licensee's duty. Provider rules for attendance records are separate. Self-study records should identify title, provider, time or credit, subject and date. Report compliance through My State Bar Profile or accepted compliance form. Personal log export can assist an audit, with no external import verified. [State Bar MCLE compliance and audit](https://www.calbar.ca.gov/legal-professionals/maintaining-compliance/mcle/mcle-compliance) · [Title 2 r. 2.73](https://www.calbar.ca.gov/legal-professionals/rules/rules-state-bar/title-2-rights-and-responsibilities-licensees)

## Proposed layout · 10 active fields

| Field | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Activity date, title and subject | Three standard fields | Maintain audit identity and self-study subject detail. |
| Elapsed duration | Standard duration | Personal time, independently of awarded credit. |
| Provider | Short text | Trace certificate and self-study activity. |
| Credit mode | Controlled selection | Participatory or self-study. |
| Awarded credit | Decimal, MCLE unit | Provider-awarded or separately verified credit, not an automatic time conversion. |
| Awarded subfield amounts | Repeating category–decimal allocation | Keep partial subfield amounts and parent/child relationship. Budget one control if separately authorised. |
| Evidence | Standard attachment | Retain attendance certificate or equivalent where applicable. |
| Activity reference | Short text | Optional official course identifier. |

The report needs separate overall, mode and subfield totals. Implicit bias contributes within the bias allocation and prevention/detection within competence. Count credit once towards total and show the allocation instead of summing every tagged field. If the release cannot handle repeated allocations, use explicit bounded per-subfield numeric fields and confirm the layout still fits the 20-field cap. Exclusion and pro-rating logic need official examples and a specialist review.

## Commercial assessment

The State Bar's 2026 report gives approximately **201,000 active licensees**, with exemptions and reachable demand unresolved. Fine-grained self-managed totals, certificate import and portable proof are promising. Course-provider records, free worksheets and future official tooling may reduce demand. The Bar issued an MCLE management system procurement Q&A in 2025; that is evidence of procurement activity, not of a launched replacement. High build/maintenance effort compared with a single hours-only scheme. Recheck official tracker status and run a matched task against the Bar's personal log before launch.
