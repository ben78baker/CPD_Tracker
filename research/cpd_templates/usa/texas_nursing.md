# United States · Texas nursing continuing competency

**Checked 20 November 2026.** Texas Board of Nursing (BON), RN/LVN renewal. **Recommendation: validate first as preparation only.** Exclude APRN and unusual renewal rules from initial scope.

## Baseline and important change

The standard continuing competency route for nurses is **20 accredited contact hours in the area of practice per two-year licensing period** or approved national certification in the relevant area, subject to topic and role rules. Jurisprudence/ethics has a separate recurrence of at least two contact hours by the end of every third two-year period. Other requirements may depend on the nurse's practice. The official renewal guidance says that **since 1 September 2026**, renewal or reactivation requires entering evidence of current continuing competency through the CE Module in the Texas Nurse Portal, with an exception for qualifying first-renewal waivers. Mandatory portal documentation materially reduces the value of a stand-alone record if it does not speed up entry. [Texas BON Education FAQ](https://nursing.texas.gov/faq_education_continuing_ed_and_competency.asp) · [Texas BON renewal information](https://bon.texas.gov/licensure_renewal.asp.html)

Original certificates and other accepted completion evidence should be retained. Random audit participants may be unable to renew before proof is approved. Live portal field list, exact retention term and audit upload behaviour were not fully checked in this pass. Some Texas BON direct detail pages timed out or returned 502, so inspect the current Board Rule 216, actual CE Module and portal before any retention, submission or coverage claim. [Texas BON continuing education guidance](https://www.bon.texas.gov/education_continuing_education.asp.html)

## Proposed layout · 10 active fields

Keep the standard date, title, activity detail, elapsed duration and attachment. Add approved provider, contact hours awarded, controlled topic/category, approval or certificate reference, and credential/eligibility note. Requiredness depends on the chosen pathway. Distinguish actual time from awarded contact hours. Store evidence and independent status, without declaring the module complete. Licence dates, mandatory topic recurrences, approved certifications and alternative pathways are portfolio data.

The October 2026 BON bulletin reports **436,300 RN, 99,976 LVN and 57,531 APRN licences**. These are licences, not unique app customers. A policy transition and strong official workflow make demand uncertain. Medium difficulty for a personal log, high for accurate configurable eligibility across roles. Validate whether employer certificates and CE Module duplicate work before taking this into a paid catalogue.
