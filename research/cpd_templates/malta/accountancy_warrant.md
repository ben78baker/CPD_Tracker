# Malta · Accountancy warrant CPE

**Checked 20 November 2026.** Accountancy Board Directive 1 warrant route. **Recommendation: defer as a paid standalone product.** A lightweight jurisdiction pack may become economical if accounting summaries are already reusable and local distribution is demonstrated.

## Duties and evidence

Unless exempt, warrant holders complete **25 structured hours per calendar year**, including **at least 10 in Core Competencies**, and **15 unstructured hours**. Hours and Core versus Professional Development categories form part of the Annual Return. Keep supporting evidence of structured participation for **five years**. The Board lists provider certificates or confirmations among accepted evidence. The general return is due **31 January** after the calendar year. Use the current official annual form or web portal. Verify year-specific extensions separately. The Malta Institute of Accountants (MIA) confirms its own membership regime has the same headline totals, but dual-status users have separate reporting obligations. [Accountancy Board Directive 1, 2016](https://accountancyboard.gov.mt/FileDownloadPage.aspx?FileID=73) · [Board CPE scheme, section 10](https://accountancyboard.gov.mt/FileDownloadPage.aspx?FileID=69) · [Board FAQs](https://accountancyboard.gov.mt/Library/FrequentlyAskedQuestions.aspx) · [MIA CPE FAQs](https://members.miamalta.org/members/faqs/cpe-faqs)

The Board's forms and the Institute's CPE services are official alternatives. Sources support maintaining own proof and declaring totals. No external import format or regulator-reviewed personal app format was established. No specific upcoming changes were identified in this pass. Verify exemptions, carry-forward and the live return against current guidance before giving personalised progress advice.

## Proposed layout · 10 active fields

Use five standard fields for date, title, learning detail, elapsed duration and evidence. Add provider, structured/unstructured designation, competency classification, credited CPE duration and provider/course reference. Structured and unstructured use disjoint annual totals. Core is a subset of structured. The record should let users explain eligibility and retain proof, with renewal declaration and exemptions at portfolio level. Distinguish applicability of Board and MIA labels. Avoid claiming regulator approval.

## Commercial assessment

The Board reported **4,023 CPA warrants and 1,779 practising certificates in auditing at 31 December 2025**. These overlap and must not be added as unique customers. An independent recorder may appeal to people combining different learning providers. Even the total warrant population is small relative to locales already under consideration. The total burden will be low to medium only if field definitions and summaries can be reused. Keep market acquisition and ongoing source maintenance explicit in any investment model.
