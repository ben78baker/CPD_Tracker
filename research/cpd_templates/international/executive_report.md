## Commercial conclusions

**Begin with the NMC and HCPC activity-log opportunities.** Both have a large sourced profession baseline and a practical role for a portable, independent log. NMC adds a specific participatory-duration requirement and Code mapping. HCPC values a learning mix, outcome reflection and evidence. Propose these for development approval after hands-on validation. ACCA and CII are the strongest accounting and financial services candidates where standalone evidence is accepted. The GDC has clearer calculations but stronger free competition and more rule-maintenance risk. NICEIC sole-trader work, CIOB membership, SRA, IET and California RN round out the ranked ten. [Ranking and audience evidence](../commercial_priorities.md) · [Ten proposed layouts and release checks](../initial_catalogue.md#ranked-first-ten--20-november-2026)

**Complex regulation is insufficient to justify a template.** RCVS, RICS and IOSH rely on their own apps or platforms. A paid companion can become duplicate entry. GPhC and Social Work England merit drafting tests only if the resulting transfer workflow beats the free alternative. New Zealand teacher renewal is a weak hours-log opportunity, and Driver CPC verification is controlled by providers. NSW teacher PD and CPA Ontario offer stronger independent-log pilots outside the UK. California and Ontario evidence is jurisdiction-specific. [Competitor comparison](competitor_comparison.md) · [International scheme sources](international_sources.md)

**Keep the £3.99/£12.99 assumptions while testing why people buy.** A meaningful template offers a reviewed setup, relevant totals, reliable evidence handling and manageable updates. Individual reusable definitions should remain easy to use via Custom Fields. A £3.99 sale produces roughly £2.33–£2.83 before refunds at the illustrated UK fee outcomes. A one-time purchase can fund only so much version monitoring and support. The assumptions and register counts give no conversion forecast. ## Product and architecture conclusions

- The [Field Library](field_library.md) contains **60 candidate definitions**, including scoped and portfolio-only items. Use text, dates, integer-minute duration and managed attachments as the foundation. Support stable single- and bounded multi-select for the leading templates, plus subset duration validation. Use exact decimal units where a scheme awards credits separately from time. These are proposed capabilities.
- An activity log is the practical first output. An audit portfolio, reflective statement, signed declaration or official portal submission requires separate handling. General PDF, evidence PDF/ZIP and CSV do not establish scheme eligibility or official import. Prioritise selected evidence, accurate period and category totals, explicit exclusions, and a reviewable handoff. [Reporting matrix](../reporting_requirements.md)
- Storage is still centred on a fixed record schema and profession names. Review stable IDs, typed values and units, immutable revisions, retention of historical choices, attachment reconciliation and recoverable migration before committing to a Phase 1 design. Keep public naming and source licences within scope. [Architecture implications](architecture_implications.md)
