## Three screened primary-source leads

Rules checked at an introductory level only. These are not detailed layout candidates.

| Scheme | Finding and opportunity | Check next |
|---|---|---|
| Singapore · Allied Health Professions Council CPE | Full and restricted registrants enter mandatory phase 1 January 2027; first affected mass renewal 2028. Standard two-year certificate 50 points including 10 core; reviewed one-year route 25 including 5 core. PRS gives the authoritative progress report after claim approval. Late claim risks indicate potential value for reminders but a competing official workflow. High confidence on stated totals and dates. [AHPC requirements, updated 17 November 2025](https://www.ahpc.gov.sg/for-professionals/registered-allied-health-professionals/continuing-professional-education-cpe/cpe-requirements/) | Verify claim caps, per-profession core mapping, evidence criteria, administrator workflow and actual delay complaints. |
| Netherlands · BIG nursing re-registration | Official English-facing sources distinguish **2,080 work-experience hours over 5 years** from a qualifying training/certificate route. This is not a simple CPD-hours sale opportunity. High confidence on the distinction, candidate rejected for current CPD product. [BIG-register nursing criteria](https://english.bigregister.nl/registration/applications-with-a-diploma-over-5-years-old/criteria-per-profession/nurse) · [BIG-register evidence](https://english.bigregister.nl/registration/applications-with-a-diploma-over-5-years-old/criteria-per-profession/nurse/work-experience-requirement) | Examine professional-register voluntary CPD separately if there is an identified audience. |
| Australia · Queensland RPEQ | A 2026 update retains 150 hours in three registration years. Similar to Engineers Australia but with a different statutory cycle and authority. High confidence in stated headline, no shared rules inferred. [Board of Professional Engineers Queensland update](https://bpeq.qld.gov.au/about-us/news-and-events/news/2026/Update-to-RPEQ-CPD-requirements) | Check current policy text and approved assessment entity allowances, exemptions and audit obligations before supporting dual-reporting. |

Other promising next targets are California accountancy, one Canadian engineering regime outside Ontario, and an EU scheme with a documented reflective portfolio. None was verified here. Do not assume national portability inside Australia, Canada or the United States.
