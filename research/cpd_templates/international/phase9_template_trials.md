# Phase 9 · profession template trial rehearsal

**22 November 2026.** This implements the seven-day trial in the isolated CPD host. Public trials require a separate enforcement decision. The production entry point still has no trial authority. Production SQLite activation, the signed migration rehearsal and published store configuration are outside this phase.

## Entitlement and recording contract

One activation per underlying template ID in the trial authority. The ledger stores the first activation and expiry as UTC timestamps exactly 168 hours apart, plus a time watermark. Versions, placements and restored professions refer to this identity. Trial state is absent from recording exports, backups and migration snapshots. Restoration cannot activate a trial. An unavailable or invalid authority cannot issue or renew access. A permanent verified purchase takes precedence, including after expiry. Original paid-app users retain their separate Custom Fields grant.

The active trial uses purchase-equivalent, template-scoped access for placements, records, evidence, eligible layout changes, reporting selections and scheme reports. It uses existing protection for canonical fields and required report choices. Standard CPD and the generic engine do not depend on trial state. The CPD host checks template access again in write transactions. A form that reaches expiry keeps its pending values in memory while it is open. It stops writing until purchase or recognised restoration restores access. It does not auto-save a final paid-layout record at expiry.

Expiry keeps records, historical meaning, evidence, existing reporting-period state, scheme reports for the saved history, ordinary PDF and CSV, and profession backup and restore. If no reporting period was previously saved, the rehearsal can produce a transient scheme report from recorded activity dates without creating a new period. The existing offline complex-script guard still applies. Standard CPD remains available via explicit creation of a separate free tile. Within-tile fallback would need a second clearly selectable creation layout with the same canonical field bindings and type-safe historical application, without disturbing the active layout or reporting periods. This phase leaves historical revisions and template placements intact and does not silently convert them.

Catalogue and preview use the existing template form and sample reports. Only the external CPD host control can activate the trial or open checkout. It shows the no-payment terms before activation, then remaining time, notices at three days and one day, and the expired purchase action. Notifications are in-app, with checks at boundaries and on app resume. The record form shares its real evidence controls with the trial and paid paths in this rehearsal. PDF and CSV handoff uses the installed exporters. The production app has no new store or OS backup configuration. ## Enforcement decision

| Option | What it can enforce | Limits and cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current isolated ledger | A SQLite transaction on one intact profile preserves activation across app restarts, concurrent requests, offline use, updated templates, tile deletion and record restore. A monotonic process clock and durable UTC watermark resist casual system-clock rollback. Known missing or malformed history fails closed. | Reinstall, app-data clearing, replacing a device or restoring an older whole-device state can remove the authority. A restarted app cannot independently know elapsed time while it was stopped. A clock set ahead can end a trial early. There is no cross-device or cross-account identity. Keep this implementation internal. |
| Apple free non-consumable per template | Apple documents a zero-price non-consumable for a timed trial. A separate permanent product retains paid ownership semantics. A verified trial transaction's original purchase timestamp and stable SKU can reconstruct its expiry for the same store account after reinstalls or replacement. | Requires approved store products and signed testing of purchase, zero charge, no payment credentials, restore, family and account changes, revocation and offline resume. Approve a trusted time strategy and verify the transaction APIs on iOS 15.6. Modern app download IDs are scoped to a store account for the app, with availability constraints. The original app's purchase date is unrelated to trial activation. [App Review Guidelines §3.1.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) · [StoreKit transaction properties](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/transaction-properties) · [App transaction ID](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/apptransaction/apptransactionid) |
| Google Play one-time purchase | A verified paid purchase and restored token can confer permanent ownership. Published one-time purchase options include buy and rent, with discount and pre-order offers. | The documentation does not establish an automatically offered, account-restorable zero-price non-consumable trial matching this design. The obfuscated account ID is supplied by the app and cannot identify the user's Google Play account. Keep previews available while testing any separately approved offer design. [One-time product options](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/one-time-product-multi-purchase-options-offers) · [Purchase lifecycle](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/lifecycle/one-time) |
| Device-local hardening | iOS device-only Keychain can protect small local ledger data. Android app-private storage and Keystore can protect local integrity. | Device-only Keychain items cannot transfer to a new device and public guarantees for uninstall recovery are insufficient. Android uninstall/data clear, no-backup storage and key lifecycle cannot provide an account ledger. `ANDROID_ID` on API 26+ is scoped to signing key, OS user and device, can change after a reset, and has a different scope on API 24. An identifier does not retain a redemption and should not be collected for this implementation. [Apple Keychain accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/security/ksecattraccessibleafterfirstunlockthisdeviceonly) · [Android ID reference](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/provider/Settings.Secure#ANDROID_ID) · [Android Auto Backup](https://developer.android.com/identity/data/autobackup) |
| Device signals or a minimal online ledger | DeviceCheck or Play Integrity can provide anti-abuse signals. A separately approved verified identity plus online atomic redemption can enforce one trial per template with a signed offline grant and trusted start. | Apple DeviceCheck has two bits per device, and Play Integrity device recall beta has three. Neither represents a per-template catalogue or a person across devices. App Attest keys renew after reinstall. An online ledger needs identity linking, availability, privacy disclosures and support. Anonymous device credentials alone do not solve replacement. Consider this only if validation justifies the infrastructure. [Apple DeviceCheck](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/devicecheck) · [App Attest key lifecycle](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/devicecheck/establishing-your-app-s-integrity) · [Play device recall](https://developer.android.com/google/play/integrity/device-recall) |

Google also supports one-time product promotion codes. The documented allowance of 500 codes per app per quarter across non-subscription promotions makes this an option for a controlled invitation exercise. It still needs code distribution, an app-enforced duration and store/policy validation for the dedicated trial product. It does not demonstrate an on-demand trial for every eligible template customer. [Play Console promotions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6321495)

Review the platform conditions above on a signed test configuration before promising one trial per user. Prefer a verified store-backed route on iOS if it meets the conditions. On Android, keep time-limited trials disabled unless an approved mechanism demonstrates comparable guarantees. Continue offering unrestricted synthetic previews on both platforms. No trial should enter the public catalogue based solely on this ledger.

## Physical-device and store checklist

- Use separately approved store test identities, separate from the existing Phase 3B signed migration rehearsal. Test iOS 15.6 and Android API 24 where available and a current physical device on each platform. No step enables production cutover, modifies release signing or uses personal professional records.
- Qualify one activation via distinct templates and versions, concurrent taps, reinstall, app-data clearing, restore to a second device on the same account, account switching, Family Sharing where applicable, older state restoration and deliberate clock jumps, DST, restart and offline checks. Verify that a known missing authority shows a retry or purchase path while historical access stays available.
- Verify terms and storefront disclosure before any transaction. Exercise free redemption without payment details, exact expiry, pending and declined transactions, payment charged only after an explicit purchase, purchase during the trial, restore after expiry, refund/revocation, store outage, original paid-app access and separate Custom Fields ownership. Use account-backed scenarios to determine real enforcement limits.
- Exercise layouts, form editors and real document/photo handoff from sandboxed providers. Leave one form open at expiry and resume it offline. Check protected fields, old values, evidence, ordinary and historic scheme exports, multiple placements, no lost records, and verified profession restore after expiry. Verify screenshot/VoiceOver/TalkBack and scaling across all locales. Qualify user-selected shares, native PDF evidence and conditional original ZIP independently of these debug tests.
- Approve Terms, Privacy Policy and product disclosures before offering trials. Reassess the retention and protection of store identifiers, trusted-time material and trial records if a platform service is selected. Do not introduce optional analytics or add an app login for this feature without approval.

## Rehearsal commands and coverage

Use `test/recording_engine/phase9_trial_rehearsal_app.dart` with an isolated debug app identity. It creates a fresh synthetic profile and a separate trial authority, and uses the existing Phase 8 profession archive and simulated checkout. `native_phase9_trials.dart` uses a disposable directory and UTC clock fixture to exercise native SQLite, entitlement-independent archive restoration and historical reports.

- **Flutter** · 265 tests passed, including seven new tests for the trial clock, notices, preview isolation, expiry with unsaved form input, purchase restore and the six locales and eight accents at large text size. Static analysis found the same 13 existing issues, with no findings in changed files.
- **Native** · Synthetic suites passed on the iOS 18.5 simulator and Android API 24 and 34 emulators. They exercised concurrent activation, offline restart, independent templates, new versions, multiple placements, deletion and restoration, paid/legacy precedence and invalid or missing trial history. An expired template still generated a historical scheme PDF and field CSV projection. Both original evidence files survived the profession backup and restore with their original digests.
- **Launchable rehearsal** · The iOS 18.5 simulator launched the isolated UI. Android API 24 completed the welcome-to-catalogue activation flow and retained the countdown after a force-stop and restart, with no second start action.
- **Remaining** · iOS 15.6, signed physical-device flows, native provider handoffs and real store transactions remain outside this verification. The device and store checklist above remains open. Legal drafts retain local review status pending the release decision.
