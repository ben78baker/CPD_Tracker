# England and Wales barrister CPD

**Scope** Practising barristers under Bar Standards Board (BSB) Handbook Part 4 C. EPP and NPP are materially different profiles.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high on current published thresholds and record duties.  
**Recommendation** Validate a shared planning and activity form with two separate reporting modes. EPP competes with a simple document.

## Rules and workflow

The **New Practitioner Programme (NPP)** spans the first three calendar years of practice as described under the scheme. It requires at least **45 hours**, including **nine advocacy** and **three ethics**. Check commencement, extension and approved compulsory course rules before counting dates or activity eligibility. The **Established Practitioners Programme (EPP)** operates in each calendar year when in scope and has no prescribed hours target. It requires a written annual plan, record of completed activities and an annual reflection covering deviations and future learning objectives, as well as an annual declaration. EPP plan and activity records must be retained for three years. The updated public guidance was published 24 August 2026 and replaces earlier EPP/NPP guidance. The BSB provides optional templates and can request records at a spot check. [BSB current guidance](https://www.barstandardsboard.org.uk/for-barristers/cpd.html) · [BSB supervision and enforcement](https://www.barstandardsboard.org.uk/for-barristers/cpd/cpd-supervision-and-enforcement.html)

The annual Authorisation to Practise process handles the official declaration for the prior CPD year. A portable written record can support supervision. No external software import verified. Protected client or case details should stay out of exported narratives. [BSB Authorisation to Practise](https://www.barstandardsboard.org.uk/for-barristers/authorisation-to-practise.html)

## Draft layout · 8 active fields

| Field | Type and use |
|---|---|
| Activity date | Standard date, calendar year or NPP window. |
| Activity title | Standard short text. |
| Activity and practice context | Standard details. |
| Time spent | Standard duration; eligibility and subtotals apply to NPP, personal for EPP. |
| Learning objective | Short text or plan linkage, useful before and after activity. |
| Advocacy/ethics designation | Conditional choice for NPP, with official eligibility confirmed by user. |
| Outcome and next steps | Long text, a source for separate EPP annual reflection. |
| Supporting evidence | Standard files, optional and confidential. |

The plan, reflections on deviation, and formal declaration belong to the annual portfolio. Keep the two regimes and transition distinct even if title, details and evidence are reused. Useful if it simplifies the audit record, with uncertain demand versus chambers tools and BSB templates. [BSB guidance](https://www.barstandardsboard.org.uk/for-barristers/cpd.html)
