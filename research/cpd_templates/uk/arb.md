# ARB architect CPD draft

**Jurisdiction** United Kingdom  
**Scope** Architects on ARB Register; RIBA member recording option  
**Recommendation** Suitable as a portal companion; lower launch priority.  
**Status** Research specification for review · 19 November 2026.

## Executive summary and official requirements

Record and reflect in MyARB or eligible RIBA platform. Fire and life safety and environmental sustainability are mandatory topics. ARB suggests aiming for eight activities per year, including one for each mandatory topic. It specifies no hours or points quota. Both formal and informal development can count. PDP conditions apply to specified entrants or rejoiners; do not require a plan field on every record. [B1 · CPD scheme](https://arb.org.uk/architect-information/cpd/) · [B2 · CPD sample records](https://arb.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/CPD-sample-records.pdf)

## Evidence, official systems and reporting

The learning reflection is the evidence. ARB advises against uploading certificates or attendance reports. Declare completion and recording location at annual retention or arrange deferment. The official permitted systems and year closure rules mean a PDF or personal draft cannot substitute. Verify RIBA obligations separately and avoid double-entry claims. [B1 · CPD scheme](https://arb.org.uk/architect-information/cpd/) · [B2 · CPD sample records](https://arb.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/CPD-sample-records.pdf)

## Proposed individual record

**6 active fields** including the standard bindings listed below. M is mandatory for the indicated scheme or submission, C is conditional, R is recommended and O is optional. Where a regulator specifies an outcome without prescribing columns, the fields are product recommendations. M/C checks should surface when marking a record ready or preparing a scheme export, with incomplete historical data remaining editable. Show all rows for eligible records unless conditional, and omit blank optional values from PDF. Export rule exceptions appear below.

| Field | Type | Class | Purpose | Standard binding | Proposed validation | Reporting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Completion date | Date | R | Attribute to recording year | date | completed date | period |
| Activity title | Text | R | Identify project learning or activity | title | short label | Detail only |
| Activity and relevance | Long text | R | Explain relevance to architectural practice | details | avoid client/project identifiers if unnecessary | Detail only |
| Mandatory topic | Multi-choice | R | Identify fire/life safety or environmental sustainability | New | allow other CPD without a mandatory topic | topic coverage |
| Learning reflection | Long text | M | Describe development and application | New | own words | Detail only |
| Duration | Duration | O | Personal tracking where desired | duration | zero allowed | personal only |

The specification derives from [B1 · CPD scheme](https://arb.org.uk/architect-information/cpd/) and [B2 · CPD sample records](https://arb.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/CPD-sample-records.pdf). The types and validation wording are design recommendations. Choice, multi-choice and decimal inputs require separate feature approval.

## App fit and export boundary

The current app has one canonical activity date and duration, title, details, and general evidence. Standard bindings preserve those semantics. Existing PDF, PDF with evidence and CSV work from a profession and date selection and do not calculate this scheme’s eligibility or assemble its portfolio. In a scheme report, export applicable activity fields and evidence filenames/references by default. Make private reflections and original attachments selectable and keep private personal tracking out of formal totals where indicated. Review the [reporting gaps](../reporting_requirements.md) before making any product claims.

## Commercial potential, confidence and outstanding checks

Lower willingness-to-pay hypothesis for an extra log when architects already use MyARB or RIBA. Copy-ready reflection and topic monitoring need to offer real value. Reassess after interviews.

Core obligations above are supported by the linked official guidance. Field layouts are proposals. Commercial confidence is provisional without audience and purchase validation. Before approval, resolve the scope and workflow questions raised above and compare a representative draft and export with the latest regulator example or portal. Avoid assuming a rule from another profession where retention, signatures, categories or exceptions remain unverified.
