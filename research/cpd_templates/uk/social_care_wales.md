# Social Care Wales continuing professional development

**Scope** Registered social care workforce in Wales under Social Care Wales guidance. Check the rules for a specific registration role and any specialist continuing professional education.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high confidence in current recording workflow, no verified blanket hours quota.  
**Recommendation** Validate if manager review and portability matter. Keep separate from England and Scotland.

## Rules and workflow

Registered workers carry out regular role-relevant development, keep a record and discuss it with a manager. Includes relevant mandatory training, with encouragement to mix types of learning. The organisation can choose a sample and ask to see records. SCWonline is an available log. The guidance says no routine sending of the record and names SCWonline as an option. It does not establish file formats, a mandatory upload for all or a blanket hours minimum. Social Care Wales announced changes on 1 April 2025, and the current recording resource was updated 9 October 2026. Consult current guidance before assuming pre-change requirements. [SCW recording guidance](https://socialcare.wales/registration/managing-your-registration/continuing-professional-development-cpd) · [SCW change notice](https://socialcare.wales/news-stories/weve-changed-our-cpd-requirements-to-make-it-simpler)
