# RICS qualified member CPD rules from 2026

**Scope** AssocRICS, MRICS and FRICS members, with exemptions handled separately. Candidate assessment rules are outside scope. UK-first distribution, with the global qualified member rule subject to applicable local obligations.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high confidence in formal rules.  
**Recommendation** Defer regular paid capture unless a tested companion solves a meaningful workflow problem.

## Rules and workflow

From 1 January 2026, qualified members ordinarily record **20 hours per calendar year, at least 10 structured**. An activity ordinarily needs 30 minutes, though similar shorter activities can be grouped. Mandatory topic coverage is rolling over three CPD years. Each topic needs structured activity of at least one hour. Record each activity’s outcomes and an annual statement about learning needs, impact and next year's plans. Annual records and declarations are due by 31 January following the activity year. A limited carry-forward rule applies to qualifying programmes, and exemptions exist. [RICS continuing professional development rules, January 2026](https://www.rics.org/content/dam/ricsglobal/documents/regulation/CPD-rules_requirements_Jan2026.pdf) · [RICS FAQs](https://www.rics.org/regulation/regulatory-compliance/continuing-professional-development/frequently-asked-questions)

RICS requires recording through **My Account or the official RICS mobile app**. Formal submission and an annual declaration complete the process. Each year a percentage of members’ records is reviewed. Check live portal category labels, exact evidence confirmation and retention before assuming they can be automated. The standard list comprises global professional and ethical standards, AI/data/technology and sustainability. Keep separate policy versions because the new rules differ from the earlier formal/informal framework. [RICS rules, Mandatory topics and annual declaration](https://www.rics.org/content/dam/ricsglobal/documents/regulation/CPD-rules_requirements_Jan2026.pdf)

## Draft layout · 9 active fields

| Field | Type and use |
|---|---|
| Activity date | Standard date; calendar-year selection. |
| Activity name | Standard text; identify the event or grouped related learning. |
| Activity and relevance | Standard details; scope and professional relevance. |
| Eligible duration | Standard duration; account for grouping and minimum duration. |
| Structured status | Required controlled choice; count the structured subtotal. |
| Mandatory topic | Conditional scheme-specific controlled choices; apply the one-hour and structured criteria. |
| Learning outcome | Required long text for each recorded activity. |
| Learning need or future action | Suggested long text to help prepare the annual statement. |
| Supporting evidence | Standard file, optional for personal evidence. |

The annual statement and declaration are not per-activity fields. Exact 2026 transition handling and any exemption calculations belong to separate versioned rules. [RICS rules](https://www.rics.org/content/dam/ricsglobal/documents/regulation/CPD-rules_requirements_Jan2026.pdf)

## Product decision

Complex rolling coverage makes a checkable summary attractive on paper. The official mobile app and member portal carry the mandatory action, so a second paid capture tool risks increasing admin. User interviews and authenticated comparisons are needed before ranking this ahead of independent-record schemes such as CII or NICEIC.
