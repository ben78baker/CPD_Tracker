# HCPC registrant activity log

**Jurisdiction** United Kingdom  
**Scope** HCPC registrants · shared scheme across its regulated professions  
**Recommendation** Suitable as a personal log with separate audit preparation.  
**Status** Research specification for review · 19 November 2026.

## Executive summary and official requirements

Keep an accurate continuous log and a mixture of at least two relevant learning types. Show how CPD sought to improve practice quality and benefit service users. The standards specify outcomes and an audit profile on request, with the previous two-year registration cycle typically relevant. Record format is flexible. The proposed fields help demonstrate standards and are not mandated form columns. [H1 · Standards of continuing professional development](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/standards/standards-of-continuing-professional-development/) · [H2 · Examples of CPD evidence](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/cpd/completing-a-cpd-profile-for-audit/cpd-evidence/)

## Evidence, official systems and reporting

Keep appropriately anonymised documents and evidence of outcomes. Certificates, learning notes and changes in practice can support selected examples. Submit the registrant’s own written profile and selected evidence during audit. A universal minimum hours, annual declaration, per-record sign-off or separate retention period was not established in these sources. [H1 · Standards of continuing professional development](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/standards/standards-of-continuing-professional-development/) · [H2 · Examples of CPD evidence](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/cpd/completing-a-cpd-profile-for-audit/cpd-evidence/)

## Proposed individual record

**8 active fields** including the standard bindings listed below. M is mandatory for the indicated scheme or submission, C is conditional, R is recommended and O is optional. Where a regulator specifies an outcome without prescribing columns, the fields are product recommendations. M/C checks should surface when marking a record ready or preparing a scheme export, with incomplete historical data remaining editable. Show all rows for eligible records unless conditional, and omit blank optional values from PDF. Export rule exceptions appear below.

| Field | Type | Class | Purpose | Standard binding | Proposed validation | Reporting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Activity date | Date | R | Chronological CPD log | date | completed date | period |
| Activity title | Text | R | Identify the activity | title | short nonblank label | Detail only |
| Activity and scope relevance | Long text | R | Connect activity with current or future practice | details | capture rationale | Detail only |
| Learning type | Choice | R | Show the portfolio learning mix | New | use research-backed category list | distinct types |
| Practice quality impact | Long text | R | Describe actual or expected practice change | New | allow lessons from unsuccessful activity | Detail only |
| Service user benefit | Long text | R | Describe intended or observed benefit | New | anonymised only | Detail only |
| Evidence | File | R | Keep selected proof and outcome material | evidence | anonymised only | Detail only |
| Time spent | Duration | O | Personal tracking | duration | zero allowed | personal only |

The specification derives from [H1 · Standards of continuing professional development](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/standards/standards-of-continuing-professional-development/) and [H2 · Examples of CPD evidence](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/cpd/completing-a-cpd-profile-for-audit/cpd-evidence/). The types and validation wording are design recommendations. Choice, multi-choice and decimal inputs require separate feature approval.

## App fit and export boundary

The current app has one canonical activity date and duration, title, details, and general evidence. Standard bindings preserve those semantics. Existing PDF, PDF with evidence and CSV work from a profession and date selection and do not calculate this scheme’s eligibility or assemble its portfolio. In a scheme report, export applicable activity fields and evidence filenames/references by default. Make private reflections and original attachments selectable and keep private personal tracking out of formal totals where indicated. Review the [reporting gaps](../reporting_requirements.md) before making any product claims.

## Commercial potential, confidence and outstanding checks

Good standalone fit across several allied health professions. A single HCPC layout is preferable to relabelled profession clones unless additional professional-body obligations are researched. Competition includes personal/employer logs and CPDme.

Core obligations above are supported by the linked official guidance. Field layouts are proposals. Commercial confidence is provisional without audience and purchase validation. Before approval, resolve the scope and workflow questions raised above and compare a representative draft and export with the latest regulator example or portal. Avoid assuming a rule from another profession where retention, signatures, categories or exceptions remain unverified.

## Independent check · 20 November 2026

The portal instructions describe a **dated activity list** entered manually or attached as one file, with Excel and Word given as examples. The profile also includes a summary of practice up to 500 words, a personal statement up to 1,500 words and separate evidence. The submission guide asks users to attach 4–12 evidence items. Profile guidance asks users to explain a break of three or more consecutive months. These are separate report objects and portal steps. A CSV or PDF can assist review; confirm format acceptance by preparing a representative sample and test via authorised access before claiming import. [HCPC profile structure](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/cy-gb/dpp/completing-a-cpd-profile-for-audit/putting-your-cpd-profile-together/) · [HCPC portal guidance](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/cpd/completing-a-cpd-profile-for-audit/submitting-your-cpd-profile-online/how-to-submit-a-cpd-profile/)
