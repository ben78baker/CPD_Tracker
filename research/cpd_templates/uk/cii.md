# Chartered Insurance Institute member CPD

**Jurisdiction** UK-first, with CII/PFS/SMB membership obligations also applying to qualified members overseas.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high confidence in core member scheme and own-format digital audit option.  
**Recommendation** Validate for the initial catalogue. Scope the product to the membership scheme and validate the audit handoff before promoting an audit export.

## Rules and workflow

Qualified CII, Personal Finance Society and Society of Mortgage Brokers members must complete at least **35 hours in a 12-month period, including 21 hours of structured CPD**. The same ordinary requirement applies to part-time members. Career breaks may suspend requirements, and categories such as eligible retired members outside insurance or advice business are exempt. The user must identify their applicable 12-month period. A separate firm, regulatory or designation obligation can also apply. [CII scheme rules](https://www.cii.co.uk/learning/cpd/scheme-rules)

The CII guidance expects activity date, name, description, structured status, development need, outcomes and reflection on application and follow-up. Duration is needed to demonstrate the 35/21 totals. The CII provides a planning and recording tool and example. Use of that tool is optional. At random audit, submit a digital record through the CII tool or by email. Employer or other professional-body records are accepted when complete. Retain evidence such as attendance certificates for production on request, although initial audit submission need not include them. An exact general retention period and a published rule review date were not established. Personal reflection is the member's own work. [CII planning and recording](https://www.cii.co.uk/learning/cpd/planning-and-recording) · [CII scheme rules](https://www.cii.co.uk/learning/cpd/scheme-rules)

FCA retail investment adviser guidance uses similar 35/21 metrics but adds the annual Statement of Professional Standing (SPS), accredited-body oversight and other role-specific CPD. Keep this a separate obligation profile. Investment, mortgage and insurance obligations must be checked individually before combining evidence or reporting. [FCA professional standards for advisers](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/professional-standards-advisers)

## Draft layout · 10 active fields

Field status reflects the member record guidance. Required history remains readable when a later rule changes.

| Field | Type and use |
|---|---|
| Activity date | Standard date; apply chosen 12-month period. |
| Activity name | Standard short text; required for identification. |
| Activity description | Standard details; brief activity type and content. |
| CPD duration | Standard duration; total eligible minutes. |
| Structured status | Controlled choice, structured/unstructured; separate subtotal. |
| Development need | Long or short text; identify the need addressed. |
| Learning outcome | Long text; show what changed in knowledge or competence. |
| Reflection and practical impact | Long text; personal account of learning and application. |
| Follow-up actions | Long text; conditional if planned, and export with reflection. |
| Supporting evidence | Standard files; optional at capture, retain if needed on request. |

Source for field mapping and optional tool availability. [CII planning and recording](https://www.cii.co.uk/learning/cpd/planning-and-recording)

## Product decision

A portable digital audit log makes this commercially stronger than schemes with a mandatory capture portal. A 35/21 dashboard, own-word prompts and a selectable evidence bundle are the expected reasons to buy. CII's tool, employer records and generic spreadsheets compete directly. The CII 2025 annual report shows about 120,000 group members across two professional membership bodies. This is an upper-bound context figure, not the number of qualified members subject to this CPD requirement. No tested upload workflow or conversion data was established. [CII annual report 2025, p. 3](https://media.umbraco.io/ciigroup-dxp/jjhojrn0/cii-annual-report-2025.pdf) Compare actual CII audit guidance and sample exports before a release claim. A 35/21 report must keep the structured subtotal within recorded CPD, avoid counting the same activity twice, and preserve unchanged evidence references.
