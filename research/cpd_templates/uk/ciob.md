# Chartered Institute of Building member CPD

**Scope** CIOB members under the February 2026 policy, with retired designations RetMCIOB and RetFCIOB excluded. Treat membership as internationally portable only where the same policy and member circumstances apply.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high confidence in policy, medium in live portal fields.  
**Recommendation** Validate for the initial catalogue after testing audit transfer.

## Rules and workflow

The February 2026 policy specifies **25 hours per subscription year**. Four named themes each require at least three hours: Health, Safety and Wellbeing, Sustainability, Quality, and Professionalism. The other 13 hours can address the member’s learning needs. A mix of formal and self-directed learning is recommended. The suggested 15/10 balance is not a category minimum. Members must also write an annual reflective statement about overall learning and future needs. The official FAQ specifies 250–450 words and confirms the updated policy applies to CPD from 2026 onwards. [CIOB FAQ](https://www.ciob.org/learning/cpd-policy) [CIOB CPD policy, February 2026, pp. 2–4](https://www.ciob.org/media/3548/download)

Members can keep their own record, use an employer system or record on the CIOB Members’ Portal. For a selected audit they must submit through the portal. The policy describes sample-based audits and calls for evidence of study and development. The report should distinguish an independent record from the required audit step. Category attribution rules for one activity spanning themes, exact retention term and authenticated import options were not established. Apply the new policy to the 2026 subscription year onwards. Keep pre-2026 records separable because they followed a different points-based approach. [CIOB policy](https://www.ciob.org/media/3548/download) · [Public CPD guidance](https://www.ciob.org/learning/cpd-policy)

Keep the annual reflective account at report or portfolio level. Avoid counting whole hours under multiple headings unless CIOB confirms that treatment. [CIOB CPD policy, pp. 2–4](https://www.ciob.org/media/3548/download)

## Product decision

The four named buckets and an independent capture option create a clearer paid benefit than generic construction CPD. Employers and the CIOB portal remain alternatives. A complementary summary could show progress by subscription year, four category subtotals, and a reflection worksheet. Ordinary record views and PDFs cannot stand in for formal audit submission. CIOB reports over 50,000 members across more than 100 countries. This is worldwide membership before exceptions and local distribution constraints. Demand and audit input friction remain unmeasured. [CIOB individual membership](https://www.ciob.org/membership)
