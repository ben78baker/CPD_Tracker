# Northern Ireland pharmacy CPD portfolio

**Scope** Pharmacists registered with the Pharmaceutical Society of Northern Ireland (PSNI). Great Britain GPhC and Ireland PSI routes are separate.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high on portfolio structure, medium on current exception deadlines and live forms.  
**Recommendation** Defer an independent submission product. Validate an offline draft companion only if pharmacists confirm enough value.

## Rules and workflow

CPD is a legal requirement and each pharmacist ordinarily submits an annual portfolio. The framework gives a CPD year ending **31 May**. A full annual portfolio has a minimum **30 hours** of activity and an approved partial portfolio has a **15-hour** minimum. Documentation time is excluded. Regulator Online (ROL) accepts hour and quarter-hour blocks. The framework’s June 2024 revision differentiates scheduled learning cycles (reflection, planning, action and evaluation) and unscheduled cycles (action and evaluation). Check the exact assessment criteria, cycles, scope exceptions and upcoming dates before calculating readiness. ROL is the normal recording and submission system. Handwritten submission is an exception where the Registrar agrees. [PSNI revised CPD framework, June 2024, §§ 2–4](https://psni.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/CPD-Framework-2021-Revised-June-2024-1.pdf) · [PSNI recording and assessment guide](https://psni.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Revised-Guide-to-CPD-Recording-Submission-and-Assessment-June-2024.pdf)

ROL validates statistical information. Submission and assessment also address the content and evidence. Files can be supporting material where appropriate. Do not equate an eligible-hour total with an assessed portfolio. Official templates and facilitation are available. Exact retention, exception deadlines, eligibility for alternative entry and authenticated transfer are open. [PSNI CPD resources](https://psni.org.uk/cpd-resources/) · [PSNI framework](https://psni.org.uk/cpd-framework/)
