# NMC revalidation CPD log

**Jurisdiction** United Kingdom  
**Scope** NMC registrants · nurses, midwives and nursing associates  
**Recommendation** Suitable with participatory-duration reporting.  
**Status** Research specification for review · 19 November 2026.

## Executive summary and official requirements

35 relevant CPD hours in the three-year registration period, including 20 participatory hours. Interaction can be virtual or in person. The required activity log covers method, topic/practice relevance, dates, total and participatory time, relevant part of the Code and evidence. The published CPD log is recommended. Five separate reflective accounts use the approved form, with reflective discussion and confirmation elsewhere in revalidation. CPD hours and practice hours are distinct. [N1 · Continuing professional development](https://www.nmc.org.uk/revalidation/requirements/cpd/) · [N2 · Written reflective accounts](https://www.nmc.org.uk/revalidation/requirements/written-reflective-accounts/) · [N3 · How to revalidate with the NMC](https://www.nmc.org.uk/globalassets/sitedocuments/revalidation/how-to-revalidate-booklet.pdf)

## Evidence, official systems and reporting

Keep participation evidence for the confirmer. Attach a certificate, agenda or anonymised record of participation where appropriate. The three-year renewal period is personal. A CPD export can support confirmation, while the approved reflective forms, declarations and NMC submission remain separate. An independent activity-level retention period was not established. [N1 · Continuing professional development](https://www.nmc.org.uk/revalidation/requirements/cpd/) · [N2 · Written reflective accounts](https://www.nmc.org.uk/revalidation/requirements/written-reflective-accounts/) · [N3 · How to revalidate with the NMC](https://www.nmc.org.uk/globalassets/sitedocuments/revalidation/how-to-revalidate-booklet.pdf)

Where a regulator specifies an outcome without prescribing columns, the fields are product recommendations. ## App fit and export boundary

The current app has one canonical activity date and duration, title, details, and general evidence. Standard bindings preserve those semantics. Existing PDF, PDF with evidence and CSV work from a profession and date selection and do not calculate this scheme’s eligibility or assemble its portfolio. In a scheme report, export applicable activity fields and evidence filenames/references by default. Make private reflections and original attachments selectable and keep private personal tracking out of formal totals where indicated. Review the [reporting gaps](../reporting_requirements.md) before making any product claims.

## Commercial potential, confidence and outstanding checks

High record specificity and an identifiable market. The official log competes directly. Offline evidence and participatory totals are the main advantages. Check that users can select the relevant Code themes and recognise the difference between a personal log and full revalidation.

Core obligations above are supported by the linked official guidance. Field layouts are proposals. Commercial confidence is provisional without audience and purchase validation. Before approval, resolve the scope and workflow questions raised above and compare a representative draft and export with the latest regulator example or portal. Avoid assuming a rule from another profession where retention, signatures, categories or exceptions remain unverified.

## Independent check · 20 November 2026

The public CPD guidance still shows 35/20 hours. The recommended log in the NMC booklet allows the relevant **part or parts** of the Code. Preserve multiple mappings where needed. The four options are Prioritise people, Practise effectively, Preserve safety, and Promote professionalism and trust. Store stable choice IDs with a Code-version reference. A compliant evidence workflow remains a separate question from form labels. [NMC CPD requirements](https://www.nmc.org.uk/revalidation/requirements/cpd/) · [NMC booklet, CPD log](https://www.nmc.org.uk/globalassets/sitedocuments/revalidation/how-to-revalidate-booklet.pdf)

Check the final consultation and effective dates before release. The product needs a dated scheme version and human review of changes. Existing records retain their original classification.
