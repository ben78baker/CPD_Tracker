# Scottish Social Services Council continuous professional learning

**Scope** Scotland · workers within the Scottish Social Services Council (SSSC) register. This report's draft layout targets experienced registered social workers.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high on annual record and statutory topic structure; check role-specific cases.  
**Recommendation** Validate an own-record and supervisor-review companion. Do not reuse the England social work portal template.

## Rules and workflow

SSSC rules require annual continuous professional learning (CPL), covering trauma awareness and protection of children and adults for registered workers. Schedule 3 specifies different core topics for social workers and officers, and social care/children and young people’s workers. Newly qualified social workers have separate mandatory activity in Schedule 4. Workers keep an annual record. The public guidance says registration includes annual confirmation, with records not routinely uploaded. Random endorsement may involve discussing learning with a supervisor. MyLearning offers tracking. No universal annual CPD hours target or retention period was established in these sources. [Registration (No. 2) Rules 2024, Schedules 3–4](https://www.sssc.uk.com/publications/downloads/2025/04/The-Scottish-Social-Services-Council-Registration-No.-2-Rules-2024-1.pdf) · [SSSC CPL guidance](https://www.sssc.uk.com/supporting-your-learning/continuous-professional-learning/)
