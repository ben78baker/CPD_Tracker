# SRA solicitor continuing competence

**Jurisdiction** England and Wales  
**Scope** Solicitors and relevant registered lawyers under SRA competence requirements  
**Recommendation** Suitable as a learning and development log.  
**Status** Research specification for review · 19 November 2026.

## Executive summary and official requirements

Maintain competence relevant to the role. Reflect, identify needs, plan and address learning, then evaluate and record it. The guidance allows other formats and expects evidence of the need and how it was identified. A time-only list is inadequate. No activity-hour quota is established by the cited recording guidance. [L1 · Recording and evaluating your learning and development](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/resources/continuing-competence/record-evaluate/) · [L2 · Learning and development templates](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/resources/continuing-competence/templates/) · [L3 · Continuing competence](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/resources/topic/continuing-competence)

## Evidence, official systems and reporting

Record application, outcomes and further steps rather than merely storing certificates. SRA can request training records. Its own learning and development templates are optional. The annual certificate renewal declaration applies to practising-certificate solicitors and the registered European or Swiss lawyer groups specified in the guidance. Registered Foreign Lawyers have competence obligations but no annual declaration under this guidance. Keep declarations outside the activity form. Confirm retention expectations before promoting a renewal-oriented workflow. ## Proposed individual record

**8 active fields** including the standard bindings listed below. M is mandatory for the indicated scheme or submission, C is conditional, R is recommended and O is optional. Where a regulator specifies an outcome without prescribing columns, the fields are product recommendations. M/C checks should surface when marking a record ready or preparing a scheme export, with incomplete historical data remaining editable. Show all rows for eligible records unless conditional, and omit blank optional values from PDF. Export rule exceptions appear below.

| Field | Type | Class | Purpose | Standard binding | Proposed validation | Reporting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Activity date | Date | R | Chronological record | date | completed date | period |
| Activity title | Text | R | Identify learning | title | short label | Detail only |
| Activity completed | Long text | R | Describe the learning undertaken | details | nonblank for completed record | Detail only |
| Need and how identified | Long text | M | Give evidence of identified learning need and its origin | New | prompt for both parts | Detail only |
| Outcome and application | Long text | R | Explain what changed or will change | New | capture actual or planned steps | Detail only |
| Further action | Long text | R | Address outstanding or new needs | New | omit if none | Detail only |
| Supporting material | File | O | Keep relevant proof or notes | evidence | exclude identifiable client information | Detail only |
| Time spent | Duration | O | Personal time tracking | duration | zero allowed | personal only |

The specification derives from [L1 · Recording and evaluating your learning and development](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/resources/continuing-competence/record-evaluate/) and [L2 · Learning and development templates](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/resources/continuing-competence/templates/) and [L3 · Continuing competence](https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/resources/topic/continuing-competence). The types and validation wording are design recommendations. Choice, multi-choice and decimal inputs require separate feature approval.

## App fit and export boundary

The current app has one canonical activity date and duration, title, details, and general evidence. Standard bindings preserve those semantics. Existing PDF, PDF with evidence and CSV work from a profession and date selection and do not calculate this scheme’s eligibility or assemble its portfolio. In a scheme report, export applicable activity fields and evidence filenames/references by default. Make private reflections and original attachments selectable and keep private personal tracking out of formal totals where indicated. Review the [reporting gaps](../reporting_requirements.md) before making any product claims.

## Commercial potential, confidence and outstanding checks

Good standalone fit because the SRA explicitly allows other formats and the activity-level fields are simple. Employer-managed plans and the free SRA worksheet compete. Validate private-practice and in-house use separately.

Core obligations above are supported by the linked official guidance. Field layouts are proposals. Commercial confidence is provisional without audience and purchase validation. Before approval, resolve the scope and workflow questions raised above and compare a representative draft and export with the latest regulator example or portal. Avoid assuming a rule from another profession where retention, signatures, categories or exceptions remain unverified.
