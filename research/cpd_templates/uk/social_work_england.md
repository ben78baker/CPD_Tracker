# Social Work England CPD draft

**Jurisdiction** England  
**Scope** Registered social workers · Social Work England renewal  
**Recommendation** Suitable as a portal companion with copy-ready text.  
**Status** Research specification for review · 19 November 2026.

## Executive summary and official requirements

Submit at least two different activities within the registration year, including at least one peer reflection. Deadline is 11:59 pm on 30 November. The portal needs activity type, short relevance description, last day of activity, learning, positive impact and CPD standards. The portal automatically selects standards 4.6 and 4.7. Guidance suggests 250–500 words each for learning and impact, and at least 250 words for the peer account. [W1 · How to record CPD](https://www.socialworkengland.org.uk/cpd/record-cpd/) · [W2 · What you need to know](https://www.socialworkengland.org.uk/9806a60b4c244ba98a311cfeb0386bfc)

## Evidence, official systems and reporting

Use anonymised narratives. A peer reflection describes the discussion and learning without naming the peer. Optional attachments are additional reflections in accepted formats; the regulator says not to upload course certificates or correspondence. Review and submit in the regulator’s online account. No hours calculation, external signature or separate retention rule established in these sources. [W1 · How to record CPD](https://www.socialworkengland.org.uk/cpd/record-cpd/) · [W2 · What you need to know](https://www.socialworkengland.org.uk/9806a60b4c244ba98a311cfeb0386bfc)

## Proposed individual record

**9 active fields** including the standard bindings listed below. M is mandatory for the indicated scheme or submission, C is conditional, R is recommended and O is optional. Where a regulator specifies an outcome without prescribing columns, the fields are product recommendations. M/C checks should surface when marking a record ready or preparing a scheme export, with incomplete historical data remaining editable. Show all rows for eligible records unless conditional, and omit blank optional values from PDF. Export rule exceptions appear below.

The specification derives from [W1 · How to record CPD](https://www.socialworkengland.org.uk/cpd/record-cpd/) and [W2 · What you need to know](https://www.socialworkengland.org.uk/9806a60b4c244ba98a311cfeb0386bfc). The types and validation wording are design recommendations. Choice, multi-choice and decimal inputs require separate feature approval.

## App fit and export boundary

The current app has one canonical activity date and duration, title, details, and general evidence. Standard bindings preserve those semantics. Existing PDF, PDF with evidence and CSV work from a profession and date selection and do not calculate this scheme’s eligibility or assemble its portfolio. In a scheme report, export applicable activity fields and evidence filenames/references by default. Make private reflections and original attachments selectable and keep private personal tracking out of formal totals where indicated. Review the [reporting gaps](../reporting_requirements.md) before making any product claims.

## Commercial potential, confidence and outstanding checks

Strong need for drafting because the regulator itself recommends preparing text separately. The official portal remains mandatory and caps the paid value of a simple clone. Copy-ready sections, reliable drafts and anonymity prompts could justify validation with users.

Core obligations above are supported by the linked official guidance. Field layouts are proposals. Commercial confidence is provisional without audience and purchase validation. Before approval, resolve the scope and workflow questions raised above and compare a representative draft and export with the latest regulator example or portal. Avoid assuming a rule from another profession where retention, signatures, categories or exceptions remain unverified.
