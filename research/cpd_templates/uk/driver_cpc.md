# Great Britain Driver CPC periodic training

**Scope** Drivers of lorries, buses and coaches in the Great Britain Driver CPC workflow. Route differences and exemptions apply, including National versus International CPC.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high on duration, route and verification workflow.  
**Recommendation** Reject a paid compliance template for this use case at initial launch.

## Official workflow and why it matters

Drivers ordinarily complete **35 hours of approved periodic training every five years**. International CPC requires International courses, whereas permitted course mixes for National CPC differ. DVSA services display verified training and the official training deadline. Approved centres are responsible for identity, attendance and upload to the national recording service. A personal certificate vault or user-entered hours log is a reference and cannot replace authorised training, provider submission or card renewal. The provider guidance was updated 19 November 2026. [GOV.UK Driver CPC training](https://www.gov.uk/driver-cpc-training) · [Check training hours](https://www.gov.uk/check-your-driver-cpc-periodic-training-hours) · [DVSA run a training course](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/run-a-driver-cpc-training-course)

No activity template is proposed. If validated for personal use only, the existing title, date, duration, notes and evidence can already capture course references. A separate numeric paid module would duplicate the authoritative free route and create risk where an unrecorded or ineligible course appears to count. Northern Ireland or provider-side functionality requires a distinct proposal.
