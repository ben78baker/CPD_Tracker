# ACCA standard unit-route CPD

**Jurisdiction** UK-first · ACCA scheme also operates internationally  
**Scope** ACCA members using the standard unit route; specialist and other routes excluded  
**Recommendation** Suitable with category subtotals and carry-forward reporting.  
**Status** Research specification for review · 19 November 2026.

## Executive summary and official requirements

Standard route is 40 units annually including at least 21 verifiable units. One unit is one hour. Relevance, ability to explain use in the workplace and evidence of undertaking learning determine verifiability. Up to 21 surplus verifiable units may carry forward to the next year, with a first-year exception. Different requirements apply to the part-time/semi-retired, approved employer and IFAC body routes. [A1 · Your guide to CPD](https://www.accaglobal.com/gb/en/member/cpd/your-guide-to-cpd.html) · [A2 · Managing your CPD](https://www.accaglobal.com/gb/en/member/cpd/managing-your-cpd.html)

## Evidence, official systems and reporting

Keep proof for verifiable units and records of non-verifiable work. For verifiable entries capture why chosen, learning and application. ACCA allows own-format or employer records and provides myACCA recorder, example log and summary. Retain records for three years under the ordinary rule; special jurisdictions and roles may require longer. Annual declaration and selection for review sit outside the activity log. [A1 · Your guide to CPD](https://www.accaglobal.com/gb/en/member/cpd/your-guide-to-cpd.html) · [A2 · Managing your CPD](https://www.accaglobal.com/gb/en/member/cpd/managing-your-cpd.html)

## Proposed individual record

**9 active fields** including the standard bindings listed below. M is mandatory for the indicated scheme or submission, C is conditional, R is recommended and O is optional. Where a regulator specifies an outcome without prescribing columns, the fields are product recommendations. M/C checks should surface when marking a record ready or preparing a scheme export, with incomplete historical data remaining editable. Show all rows for eligible records unless conditional, and omit blank optional values from PDF. Export rule exceptions appear below.

| Field | Type | Class | Purpose | Standard binding | Proposed validation | Reporting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Activity date | Date | R | Attribute to CPD year | date | completed date | period |
| Activity title | Text | R | Identify learning | title | short label | Detail only |
| Activity summary | Long text | R | Describe activity | details | brief summary | Detail only |
| CPD time | Duration | M | Record one hour per unit | duration | nonnegative minutes | total units |
| Verifiability | Choice | M | Segregate verifiable and non-verifiable | New | confirm qualifying evidence and relevance | category subtotal |
| Why selected | Long text | C | Explain relevance for verifiable CPD | New | nonblank for verifiable | Detail only |
| What I learned | Long text | C | Describe outcome for verifiable CPD | New | nonblank for verifiable | Detail only |
| Application | Long text | C | Explain how learning was or will be used | New | nonblank for verifiable | Detail only |
| Supporting evidence | File | C | Keep proof of participation for verifiable activity | evidence | file or traceable evidence reference | Detail only |

## App fit and export boundary

The current app has one canonical activity date and duration, title, details, and general evidence. Standard bindings preserve those semantics. Existing PDF, PDF with evidence and CSV work from a profession and date selection and do not calculate this scheme’s eligibility or assemble its portfolio. In a scheme report, export applicable activity fields and evidence filenames/references by default. Make private reflections and original attachments selectable and keep private personal tracking out of formal totals where indicated. Review the [reporting gaps](../reporting_requirements.md) before making any product claims.

## Commercial potential, confidence and outstanding checks

Commercially attractive because requirements are differentiated and own format is explicitly accepted. Official tooling competes. Avoid generic marketing to all accountants or treatment of specialist audit obligations as covered.

Core obligations above are supported by the linked official guidance. Field layouts are proposals. Commercial confidence is provisional without audience and purchase validation. Before approval, resolve the scope and workflow questions raised above and compare a representative draft and export with the latest regulator example or portal. Avoid assuming a rule from another profession where retention, signatures, categories or exceptions remain unverified.
