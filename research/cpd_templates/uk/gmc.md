# GMC doctor CPD and appraisal companion

**Scope** Doctors with a licence to practise in the UK, using the GMC doctor revalidation pathway. Specialty and designated-body guidance can add specific expectations. Physician associates and anaesthesia associates have distinct registration scope even where shared guidance exists.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high confidence in GMC CPD and appraisal rules.  
**Recommendation** Defer a generic premium template. Evaluate a focused specialty after checking its college framework and designated-body workflow.

## Rules and workflow

Doctors must maintain competence and collect CPD relevant to their complete scope of UK practice. They must reflect on suitable supporting information and discuss learning at annual appraisal. The GMC does **not** mandate a specific number of points for revalidation; college recommendations and specialty requirements need separate profiles. CPD is one of six supporting information types, and the responsible officer or suitable person ordinarily uses the full record and appraisal material for a recommendation. The usual five-year recommendation cycle can vary with individual revalidation dates and circumstances. The GMC makes the decision. [GMC CPD supporting information](https://www.gmc-uk.org/registration-and-licensing/managing-your-registration/revalidation/guidance-on-supporting-information-for-revalidation/continuing-professional-development) · [GMC revalidation requirements for doctors](https://www.gmc-uk.org/registration-and-licensing/managing-your-registration/revalidation/guidance-for-doctors-requirements-for-revalidation/revalidation-requirements-for-doctors) · [GMC recommendation workflow](https://www.gmc-uk.org/registration-and-licensing/managing-your-registration/revalidation/guidance-for-doctors-requirements-for-revalidation/when-and-how-the-revalidation-recommendation-is-made)

Advice to doctors without a responsible officer or suitable person includes retaining information discussed at appraisals since the previous revalidation. Do not extrapolate a universal retention period from that route. Check the appraisal system and the user’s role before exporting and exclude patient identifiers and unnecessary confidential detail. No universal GMC import or approved personal app verified. [GMC additional requirements for doctors without a connection](https://www.gmc-uk.org/registration-and-licensing/managing-your-registration/revalidation/guidance-for-doctors-requirements-for-revalidation/additional-requirements-for-doctors-without-a-connection)

## Draft layout · 8 active fields

| Field | Type and use |
|---|---|
| Completed date | Standard date, appraisal year context. |
| Activity title | Standard short text. |
| Activity and practice scope | Standard details, show clinical or professional relevance without identifiers. |
| Time spent | Standard duration, informational for base GMC scope. |
| Development need or plan reference | Suggested text, keep the actual PDP at portfolio level. |
| Learning and reflection | Long text, capture the professional outcome. |
| Application or improvement | Long text, describe change to practice and further action. |
| Evidence reference | Standard attachment, optional and selectively exportable. |

The layout supplies documents and narrative for appraisal but does not deliver the other revalidation evidence categories, recommendation or declarations. Avoid a default 50-point threshold for all doctors. Establish the selected college’s alternatives and whether a current employer portfolio already captures the same material before investing.
