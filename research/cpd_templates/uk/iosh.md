# IOSH membership CPD and Blueprint

**Scope** IOSH Technical, Certified, Chartered and Chartered Fellow members. UK-first, with grade requirements applying across the relevant membership where permitted.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high confidence in official grade rules and audit guidance.  
**Recommendation** Defer unless practitioners demonstrate enough offline drafting value to justify duplicate capture.

## Rules and workflow

These grades ordinarily require **30 hours over the membership year**, beginning on joining or renewal. The official guidance gives a reduced initial requirement for membership grade of less than six months. Chartered Members and Fellows also need ethical practice e-learning. IOSH requires activity recording in **Blueprint** and reviews selected members’ record there. Self-assessment and a development plan can help prepare against the competency framework. If selected for audit, the guidance provides a 90-day response window. An external file does not substitute for Blueprint in the official flow. [IOSH member CPD requirements](https://prod.iosh.com/qualifications-and-courses/cpd) · [IOSH CPD audits](https://prod.iosh.com/qualifications-and-courses/cpd/understanding-cpd-audits)

Audit guidance lists a title, category, description, hours, reflective statement and start/end dates. Reflection addresses activity, choice, learning and impact. The source establishes evidence and authentic reflection as part of audit. Confirm exact transferable evidence formats and retention directly before a release. General IOSH membership totals would not isolate the relevant grades or the customers underserved by Blueprint. [IOSH CPD audits, tips to prepare your record](https://prod.iosh.com/qualifications-and-courses/cpd/understanding-cpd-audits)

## Draft layout · 8 active fields

| Field | Type and use |
|---|---|
| End date | Standard completion date for membership cycle. |
| Start date | Required second date for an IOSH draft; no later than end date. |
| Activity title | Standard short text. |
| Category | Required choice once official choices verified. |
| Brief description | Standard details. |
| CPD duration | Standard duration, 30-hour target for applicable grades. |
| Reflective statement | Required long text, include reason, learning and application. |
| Supporting evidence | Standard file; keep optional locally pending audit conditions. |

The personalised year, annual ethical practice course and development plan are profile or portfolio items. Do not claim audit compliance from an activity-only record. [IOSH CPD and audit guidance](https://prod.iosh.com/qualifications-and-courses/cpd/understanding-cpd-audits)
