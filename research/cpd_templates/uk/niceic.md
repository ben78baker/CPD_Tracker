# NICEIC certified business CPD record

**Scope** NICEIC-certified electrical businesses, particularly sole traders recording their own learning. Separate from registration for engineers, an employment-wide trade requirement, and other certification providers. Confirm coverage of the purchaser’s exact scheme and location.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high confidence in the scheme operator’s published recording workflow.  
**Recommendation** Validate a personal sole-trader companion. A team compliance product would require separate scope and capabilities.

## Rules and workflow

NICEIC assessment guidance requires the certified business to maintain CPD records for its employed persons. The definition covers temporary, agency and subcontracted workers and can include sole traders. NICEIC expects activity, key learnings and time, with formal and informal relevant development accepted. Its CPD hub says there is no set hours quota. The business can use an existing system, and NICEIC publishes an editable record sheet. The obligation is evidence at assessment, not a personal national hours target. [NICEIC assessment guidance](https://niceic.com/for-the-trades-1/professional-standards/your-assessment/) · [NICEIC CPD hub](https://niceic.com/for-the-trades-1/develop-your-skills/) · [Recording CPD](https://niceic.com/for-the-trades-1/develop-your-skills/recording-cpd/)

NICEIC's competence guidance also covers qualifications, training, experience and risk-proportionate supervision. This is broader than activity CPD. Assessment guidance mentions at least six years for business records including competence records, subject to contractual requirements. Confirm exactly how this applies to CPD-only files and particular certification schemes. Local data retention, export and recovery matter. Keep a training certificate separate from evidence that the person was actually assessed as competent to carry out a scope of work. [NICEIC competence guidance, pp. 1–2](https://niceic.com/NicEic/media/PDF-s/assessment%20downloads/Additional-Guidance-for-Demonstrating-Competence-of-EP.pdf) · [NICEIC assessment guidance](https://niceic.com/for-the-trades-1/professional-standards/your-assessment/)

## Draft layout · 8 active fields

| Field | Type and significance |
|---|---|
| Activity date | Standard date; chronology and assessment window. |
| Activity title | Standard short text; identify the activity. |
| Activity and job relevance | Standard details; describe practical context. |
| Time spent | Standard duration; informational total, no built-in hours target. |
| Key learning | Required long text for an assessment-oriented record. |
| Applied change | Optional long text for resulting practice improvement. |
| Work scope or training category | Optional text or controlled choice after assessment testing. |
| Supporting material | Standard files; optional access to evidence such as certificate or course notes. |

The date, activity, learning and time should be prominent. Team members or supervisors need separate identity and authorisation if the product later covers several employed persons. A profession tile is a layout container and should not become an employee surveillance or verification system by accident. [NICEIC recording guidance](https://niceic.com/for-the-trades-1/develop-your-skills/recording-cpd/)

## Product decision

Good app fit for mobile personal capture and a portable assessment record. The free sheet, company LMS and existing certification documents are credible substitutes. Interview sole traders and a qualified supervisor about assessment-day needs before promising acceptance. Do not generalise the rule to every electrician or to NAPIT without scheme-specific research. NICEIC reports over 40,000 certified businesses as at 31 December 2023. The relevant sole trader fraction and pool of reachable purchasers are unverified.
