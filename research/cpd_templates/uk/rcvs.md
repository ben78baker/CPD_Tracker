# RCVS veterinary surgeons and nurses

**Scope** Practising RCVS veterinary surgeons and registered veterinary nurses. Non-practising veterinary surgeons are exempt; official pause and other individual exceptions apply.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high confidence in the annual rules and required 1CPD workflow.  
**Recommendation** Defer a paid template. One shared form with role-specific targets suffices if later demand justifies a draft companion.

## Rules and workflow

Veterinary surgeons ordinarily need **35 hours**, veterinary nurses **15 hours**, per calendar year, with reflection. CPD must suit the member’s practice and development and can include structured and informal learning. The same requirement applies to part-time practitioners. Hours do not carry forward, and only reflected activity counts. The cycle is plan, do, record and reflect. RCVS has a formal pause route. Some webinars embedded on the regulator page discuss past COVID reductions, so use the current requirements and policy sections for target logic. [RCVS CPD requirements and policies](https://www.rcvs.org.uk/veterinary-professionals/learning-and-development/continuing-professional-development-cpd) · [RCVS veterinary surgeon policy](https://www.rcvs.org.uk/sites/default/files/2026-03/cpd-policy-23.pdf)

Use **1CPD** for the official record. RCVS supplies iOS, Android and web access with the MyRCVS account, plus plans, record capture and reflection. Policy supports monitoring. Confirm current evidence attachment, record export and exact retention features with a live walkthrough. Do not treat certificates as universally required, and do not accept official record imports without verification. [RCVS 1CPD access, recording and reflection guidance](https://www.rcvs.org.uk/veterinary-professionals/learning-and-development/continuing-professional-development-cpd)

## Draft layout · 8 active fields

| Field | Type and use |
|---|---|
| Completion date | Standard date; apply calendar year. |
| Activity title | Standard text; identify learning. |
| Activity and relevance | Standard details; explain relevance to role or plan. |
| Time | Standard duration; track recorded and reflected subtotals distinctly. |
| Learning objective | Suggested short text or reference to the plan. |
| Reflection | Long text; required to count the activity. Capture what was learnt. |
| Impact on work | Suggested long text prompt within the reflection workflow. |
| Supporting evidence | Standard file attachment, optional where useful. |

The separate role profile determines 35/15 hours. A draft or ordinary PDF is a personal aid, and the user transfers the record to 1CPD. [RCVS CPD guidance](https://www.rcvs.org.uk/veterinary-professionals/learning-and-development/continuing-professional-development-cpd)

## Product decision

The regulator’s own mobile and web app does the essential job and creates duplicate-entry friction. Reported reach is not demand: RCVS Facts shows 31,224 UK practising surgeons on 1 January 2025, and committee papers show 24,823 veterinary nurses registered on 1 February 2025. These are separate counts with different dates and populations. Look for substantiated unmet needs such as portable evidence or cross-role logging before building. [RCVS Facts 2024, CPD section](https://www.rcvs.org.uk/news-and-views/publications/rcvs-facts-2024/rcvs-facts-2024f.pdf) · [Veterinary Nurses Council February 2025 figures](https://www.rcvs.org.uk/sites/default/files/2026-01/vnc-feb-25-agenda-and-papers-unclassified.pdf)
