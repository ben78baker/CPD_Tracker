# Scotland teacher Professional Update

**Scope** Teachers within GTC Scotland's Professional Update (PU) requirements. Employer-led routes and individual registration circumstances may differ.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high on requirements and sign-off, medium on employer tooling.  
**Recommendation** Validate a reflective-log companion with Scottish teachers. Defer any sign-off feature.

## Rules and workflow

Engagement is a condition of GTC Scotland registration. Teachers undertake ongoing professional learning, maintain a reflective record and evidence of impact, and discuss learning with a reviewer through Professional Review and Development (PRD). Every **five years** they confirm engagement. In the sign-off academic year GTC Scotland contacts registrants around 1 September, and confirmation is due by **1 July**. The teacher and line manager each confirm engagement, record/reflection and the PRD discussion. Sign-off is in the system specified for the employer or role, including Gateway CPD Manager or MyGTCS for relevant users. Ask the employer which system applies. The guidance reviewed does not prescribe an annual hours quota, universal field schema, a retention duration or an independent-file import. [GTC Scotland, Key elements of Professional Update](https://www.gtcs.org.uk/knowledge-base/articles/key-elements-of-professional-update)

## Draft layout · 8 active fields

| Field | Type and use |
|---|---|
| Activity date | Standard date, supports chronological PRD evidence. |
| Activity title | Standard short text; identify learning. |
| Learning activity and context | Standard details; log source and relevance. |
| Time spent | Standard duration, optional personal context. |
| Professional Standard | Controlled standards reference when scheme list verified. |
| Critical reflection | Long text; reflection on learning against the appropriate standard. |
| Impact and next steps | Long text; capture changes in thinking/practice and actions. |
| Impact evidence | Standard attachment, where useful and appropriately anonymised. |

PRD discussion and declarations are review-cycle objects. Keep employer and third-party learner data out of unneeded exports. A personal log can help prepare for review while the formal workflow remains in the relevant employer system. Check authenticated requirements, comparator prices, next review date and eligible counts before positioning.
