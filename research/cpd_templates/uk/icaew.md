# ICAEW CPD activity record

**Jurisdiction** UK-first · ICAEW members within the regulations  
**Scope** ICAEW · role-dependent categories and exemptions  
**Recommendation** Suitable with category targets and ethics reporting.  
**Status** Research specification for review · 19 November 2026.

## Executive summary and official requirements

ICAEW requires annual logging and uses role-sensitive CPD categories. The year runs 1 November–31 October. Assess each concurrent role and use the higher requirement, reviewing on role change. The record guidance covers learning needs, planned learning, ethics, evidence and reflections. Use the official scope calculator before selecting targets. ICAEW’s scope tool also lists an ethics-and-reflect route requiring an hour of ethics training and reflection on development needs rather than a general time quota. A complete category-threshold crosswalk and exemption map remain open. [Q1 · Continuing Professional Development](https://www.icaew.com/membership/cpd) · [Q2 · Recording CPD](https://www.icaew.com/membership/cpd/your-guide-to-cpd/recording-cpd) · [Q3 · Find out your CPD requirements](https://www.icaew.com/membership/cpd/your-guide-to-cpd/cpd-are-you-in-scope)

## Evidence, official systems and reporting

Keep evidence of completion for verifiable hours and prepare a record for Quality Assurance monitoring if selected. Record format can be a spreadsheet, employer system or the ICAEW digital log. That digital log integrates AddCPD verification for ICAEW activities. A full annual record needs planning and reflection across activities. Confirm statutory auditors, firm obligations, retention and declaration details for each targeted category. ## Proposed individual record

**8 active fields** including the standard bindings listed below. M is mandatory for the indicated scheme or submission, C is conditional, R is recommended and O is optional. Where a regulator specifies an outcome without prescribing columns, the fields are product recommendations. M/C checks should surface when marking a record ready or preparing a scheme export, with incomplete historical data remaining editable. Show all rows for eligible records unless conditional, and omit blank optional values from PDF. Export rule exceptions appear below.

| Activity title | Text | R | Identify activity | title | short label | Detail only |

## App fit and export boundary

The current app has one canonical activity date and duration, title, details, and general evidence. Standard bindings preserve those semantics. Existing PDF, PDF with evidence and CSV work from a profession and date selection and do not calculate this scheme’s eligibility or assemble its portfolio. In a scheme report, export applicable activity fields and evidence filenames/references by default. Make private reflections and original attachments selectable and keep private personal tracking out of formal totals where indicated. Review the [reporting gaps](../reporting_requirements.md) before making any product claims.

## Commercial potential, confidence and outstanding checks

Plausible second accounting template because the ethics/role-year workflow differs from ACCA. Strong competition from the digital log. Test demand for logging across external providers and employer transitions.

Core obligations above are supported by the linked official guidance. Field layouts are proposals. Commercial confidence is provisional without audience and purchase validation. Before approval, resolve the scope and workflow questions raised above and compare a representative draft and export with the latest regulator example or portal.
