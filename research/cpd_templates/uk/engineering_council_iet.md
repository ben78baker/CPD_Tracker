# Engineering Council and IET CPD

**Jurisdiction** United Kingdom  
**Scope** Engineering Council registrants; IET member overlay assessed separately  
**Recommendation** Suitable as an IET-specific pilot or an Engineering Council log with explicit profile choice.  
**Status** Research specification for review · 19 November 2026.

## Executive summary and official requirements

CEng, IEng, EngTech and ICTTech registrants record activities, reflect, evaluate against objectives and regularly review plans. Licensed institutions sample records annually. The Council code has no time minimum. IET guidance sets 30 hours per calendar year for professionally active members and 10 for partially active members and describes mandatory audit sampling. These are IET rules, not a threshold for all engineers or electrical installers. [E1 · Continuing Professional Development](https://www.engc.org.uk/resources-and-guidance/professional-development/continuing-professional-development-cpd) · [E2 · Continuing Professional Development](https://www.theiet.org/career/professional-development/continuing-professional-development)

## Evidence, official systems and reporting

Use an activity chronology with outputs and links to planned objectives. Written evidence or supporting documents may be requested in audit. IET provides Career Manager. Verify exact audit submission requirements and retention for the chosen institution. No universal per-activity supervisor signature was established. Report against calendar year for IET, with plans and objectives outside individual activity totals. [E1 · Continuing Professional Development](https://www.engc.org.uk/resources-and-guidance/professional-development/continuing-professional-development-cpd) · [E2 · Continuing Professional Development](https://www.theiet.org/career/professional-development/continuing-professional-development)

## Proposed individual record

**11 active fields** including the standard bindings listed below. M is mandatory for the indicated scheme or submission, C is conditional, R is recommended and O is optional. Where a regulator specifies an outcome without prescribing columns, the fields are product recommendations. M/C checks should surface when marking a record ready or preparing a scheme export, with incomplete historical data remaining editable. Show all rows for eligible records unless conditional, and omit blank optional values from PDF. Export rule exceptions appear below.

| Field | Type | Class | Purpose | Standard binding | Proposed validation | Reporting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Completion date | Date | R | Chronological activity log | date | completed date | calendar period |
| Activity start date | Date | C | Mirror IET activity dates for a date range | New | no later than completion | Detail only |
| Planned target date | Date | C | Record the IET target date for a planned activity | New | may be before or after completion | Detail only |
| Activity title | Text | R | Identify learning | title | short label | Detail only |
| Activity and context | Long text | R | Record what happened | details | brief role context | Detail only |
| Learning method | Choice | R | Describe development approach | New | include mentoring and workplace learning | mix |
| Objective or development need | Text | R | Connect to the plan where applicable | New | link or short description | Detail only |
| Learning and evaluation | Long text | R | Describe learning and evaluation against objective | New | allow unplanned learning | Detail only |
| Next steps | Long text | R | Inform plan review | New | optional draft | Detail only |
| Time spent | Duration | C | Track personal time and IET member totals | duration | zero allowed for unfinished draft | IET subtotal |
| Evidence | File | O | Keep written or external supporting proof | evidence | allow external reference | Detail only |

The specification derives from [E1 · Continuing Professional Development](https://www.engc.org.uk/resources-and-guidance/professional-development/continuing-professional-development-cpd) and [E2 · Continuing Professional Development](https://www.theiet.org/career/professional-development/continuing-professional-development). The types and validation wording are design recommendations. Choice, multi-choice and decimal inputs require separate feature approval.

## App fit and export boundary

The current app has one canonical activity date and duration, title, details, and general evidence. Standard bindings preserve those semantics. Existing PDF, PDF with evidence and CSV work from a profession and date selection and do not calculate this scheme’s eligibility or assemble its portfolio. In a scheme report, export applicable activity fields and evidence filenames/references by default. Make private reflections and original attachments selectable and keep private personal tracking out of formal totals where indicated. Review the [reporting gaps](../reporting_requirements.md) before making any product claims.

## Commercial potential, confidence and outstanding checks

Strong engineering wedge because reflection and objective evaluation are reusable, but one undifferentiated engineering template cannot imply universal requirements. Validate overlap with IET Career Manager users and employer systems.

Core obligations above are supported by the linked official guidance. Field layouts are proposals. Commercial confidence is provisional without audience and purchase validation. Before approval, resolve the scope and workflow questions raised above and compare a representative draft and export with the latest regulator example or portal. Avoid assuming a rule from another profession where retention, signatures, categories or exceptions remain unverified.

## Independent check · 20 November 2026

IET's mandatory audit guidance explicitly permits submission by email or post as alternatives to Career Manager, with possible follow-up and slower processing. It lists activity name and type, target date if planned, a start and end date, description and reflection, and CPD hours. The draft now includes optional start and target dates and keeps completion as the canonical activity date. Preserve plans separately from completed records for reporting. [IET Mandatory CPD Audit](https://www.theiet.org/career/professional-development/continuing-professional-development/mandatory-cpd-audit)
