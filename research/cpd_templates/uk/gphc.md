# GPhC pharmacy revalidation draft companion

**Scope** Pharmacists and pharmacy technicians registered with GPhC in Great Britain. Northern Ireland and Ireland are separate.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high confidence in published annual record types, transfer boundary and 2026 standards change.  
**Recommendation** Validate before building. A paid product needs to reduce writing and transfer effort for three distinct record types.

## Rules and workflow

The annual revalidation portfolio normally contains **four CPD records, at least two planned, one peer discussion and one reflective account**. It follows the individual’s renewal, with records normally covering the preceding registration year. A CPD entry may be drafted outside the official service. Final submission is electronic through myGPhC at renewal. This is an activity and record scheme, with no general annual hours quota in the framework. Peer and reflective records have their own prompts and review criteria. [GPhC revalidation framework, January 2018, pp. 8–13 and Appendix 1](https://assets.pharmacyregulation.org/files/document/gphc_revalidation_framework_january_2018.pdf) · [GPhC revalidation and renewal](https://tnaqa.mirrorweb.com/ukgwa/20261102064635oe_/https%3A/www.pharmacyregulation.org/pharmacists/revalidation-renewal)

The framework permits original local drafts and describes review sampling, targeted review and possible contact with the peer to confirm participation. Keep reflection original and avoid identifiable service-user data. From **1 January 2026**, registrants can select from any of nine professional standards for their reflective account. Check exact current selection semantics in the form. Earlier restricted standard lists must not be used as defaults. The annual report says this is part of a multi-year revalidation review continuing during 2026–27. Confirm the live form and current retention guidance before release. [GPhC annual report 2025–26, Revalidation](https://assets.pharmacyregulation.org/files/2026-10/GPhC-annual-report-2025-26a.pdf) · [GPhC framework, Review and Appendix 1](https://assets.pharmacyregulation.org/files/document/gphc_revalidation_framework_january_2018.pdf)

## Draft activity layout · 8 active fields

| Field | Type and use |
|---|---|
| Completion date | Standard date; relate to revalidation year. |
| Activity title | Standard short text; retrieval label. |
| Planned or unplanned | Required controlled choice; count four total and two planned separately. |
| Learning and role relevance | Standard details; planned learning and why, or describe unplanned learning. |
| Learning method or action | Conditional long text for planned learning. |
| Example of benefit to people using services | Required long text; describe concrete benefit. |
| Supporting material | Standard file, optional and private by default. |
| Time spent | Standard duration, optional personal measure. |

Separate draft forms or record kinds would be needed for peer discussion and the annual reflective account. Keep peer contact details out of generic exports and organisation-branded reports unless explicitly selected for the authorised purpose. [GPhC framework, Appendix 1](https://assets.pharmacyregulation.org/files/document/gphc_revalidation_framework_january_2018.pdf)

## Product decision

Four tracked activities and two portfolio artifacts cannot be represented accurately by a uniform hour dashboard. The official portal can hold drafts and already provides the final submission route. Test if reusable prompts, local privacy and quick manual transfer provide enough benefit to pay. No official bulk import verified. The regulator reports 67,321 pharmacists and 28,100 pharmacy technicians at 31 March 2026; both are broad register counts. [GPhC annual report 2025–26, p. 5](https://assets.pharmacyregulation.org/files/2026-10/GPhC-annual-report-2025-26a.pdf) Keep a public explainer and examples separate from embedded regulation claims.
