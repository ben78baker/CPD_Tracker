## Access and interpretation limits

- General web searches returned empty results during this pass. Direct source navigation and regulator links provided the findings.
- The NMC Word log and New Zealand Council Word template could not be parsed through the browser. Accessible regulator guidance and audit documents underpin the proposed fields. Verify both downloadable forms before claiming parity.
- Australia’s NMBA/Ahpra standards returned HTTP 403. Several other links returned an unavailable or timeout response. The index marks entries without adequate current primary evidence as leads.
- CORU’s physiotherapy landing page links to a PDF with a draft filename. The credit and submission findings use the accessible audit page. Confirm the Board document’s status and current activity-level instructions.
- Older guidance is used only alongside an active regulator page, with precedence given to current scheme guidance. Publication dates are included where established. Source completeness varies by scheme.
