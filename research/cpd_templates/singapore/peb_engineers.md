# Singapore · Professional Engineers Board practising certificate CPD

**Checked 20 November 2026.** Registered professional engineers seeking the standard annual practising certificate. **Recommendation: defer.** Split the standard route from return-to-practice, specialist and waiver cases.

The Board's February 2026 guidance sets **40 PDUs** per qualifying period, including at least **20 structured**. Eligible structured and unstructured activities can contribute to the rest, subject to different rates and category caps. The published examples include different awards per contact hour, for delivering learning, for other contributions and for selected self-study. An introductory sentence describing unstructured activities is ambiguous alongside the explicit credit schedule. Use the qualifying category table and confirm edge cases with PEB rather than assigning zero to all unstructured activity. The older official-hosted rules reference a qualifying period running up to 1 November. The current statute page direct fetch was blocked. Check the live rules and portal periods before hard-coding dates, lapse thresholds or retention. [PEB programme](https://www1.peb.gov.sg/continuing-professional-development/) · [PEB guidance, February 2026](https://www1.peb.gov.sg/files/About_CPD_write_up_Feb_2026.pdf) · [Singapore Statutes Online, r. 14A](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL-Rev/PEA1991-R1/Published?DocDate=20250530&ProvIds=pr14A-)

Random audit evidence can include diary records/activity log, enrolment records, receipts, certificates, attendance lists, employer statements or statutory declarations. The regulator's practising certificate renewal remains an official workflow. No third-party import and no published mandatory form for personal logging were verified. [PEB guidance, Audit Process](https://www1.peb.gov.sg/files/About_CPD_write_up_Feb_2026.pdf) · [PEB annual registration](https://www1.peb.gov.sg/register/)

## Proposed layout · 10 active fields

Use five standard fields (date, title, scope relevance, duration, attachment). Add provider, activity/code reference, structured status, versioned PEB category and credited PDU amount. An audit report could show date, category, elapsed time, claimed credits and evidence. Keep subject relevance, rule caps and portal-confirmed awards distinguishable, with no automatic conversion from raw duration. Keep any declaration at portfolio level.
