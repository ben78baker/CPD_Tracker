# Singapore · SNB nursing and midwifery continuing education

**Checked 20 November 2026.** Singapore Nursing Board (SNB), Registered Nurses, Enrolled Nurses and midwives on the applicable registration routes. **Recommendation: defer until users demonstrate material benefit in preparing claims.** Verified primary guidance for the transition and normal points targets. APN and grace-period cases require separate treatment.

## Rules and transition

The qualifying period normally runs **1 September–31 August**. Mandatory points start with practising certificate renewal **for 2028**, associated with 1 September 2026–31 August 2027. The current SNB page confirms that insufficient points do not block 2027 renewal. The FAQ specifies **15 CPE points for RNs** and **10 for ENs and midwives on a single register**. APNs normally require 30 with a specialty subset. Temporary/provisional registration and late fulfilment have specific rules. The dated FAQ's first transition example has an apparent 30 August typo. Prefer the live guidance and its general 31 August definition. Verify any exception with the Board. [SNB nurse/midwife page, 6 November 2026](https://www.snb.gov.sg/for-professionals/for-nurses-and-midwives/continuing-professional-education/for-nurse-midwife/) · [SNB mandatory CPE FAQs, pp. 1–3](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/78/1e1c18cd-b9a6-4019-9095-f2817c160cff/cpe-_faqs_6-march-2025.pdf)

Providers submit eligible approved events. Nurses/midwives can submit specified claims through the Professional Registration System (PRS) and use its points report. Event types have different award rates and caps. A local record could prepare claims and preserve proof. It cannot award points or replace PRS. Where points are unnecessary, the FAQ explicitly permits a personal or institution-based record of learning hours. An independent formal audit format and evidence retention term were not verified. [SNB criteria, revised August 2024](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/78/79a3f7f2-b9ff-4332-874a-75c41807bdfd/cpe-award-criteria-aug-2024.pdf) · [SNB PRS submission guide, May 2025](https://file.go.gov.sg/snb-step-by-step-guide-for-nurses-midwives-to-submit-cpe-claims-may-2025.pdf)

## Proposed layout · 11 active fields

Use date, title, practice relevance/learning notes, duration and attachment as the five standard fields. Add provider, official activity reference, SNB CPE category, points claimed, points awarded, and claim status. Numeric values are separate from actual duration. Keep certificate type and registration status at the level of the scheme selection. Link the alleged award to PRS confirmation where available. Reconcile accredited-provider events with personal claims to prevent duplication.

The 2024 SNB report recorded **50,389 nurses and midwives** on the Register and Roll. That total includes people outside the initial target. Claims preparation might appeal to independent learners but PRS already gives an authoritative report at the place where renewal happens. High build and maintenance burden for category caps and claims rules. A low-cost neutral personal log is a better fallback if interviews reveal little portal friction.
