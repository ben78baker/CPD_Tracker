# Canada · CPA Ontario member CPD

**Checked 20 November 2026.** CPA Ontario members under Regulation 7-2. **Recommendation: validate first, strong shortlist candidate.** Separate public accounting licences (PAL), exemptions and onboarding rules require their own treatment.

## Obligations and actual workflows

Standard requirements are **20 hours each January–December, including 10 verifiable**, and **120 over each rolling three calendar years, including 60 verifiable and four verifiable professional ethics hours within that 60**. Relevant, quantifiable learning with substantial intellectual or practical content can qualify. Eligible evidence matters more than provider branding. The annual declaration for the previous year is submitted with dues in My Portal by **1 June**. Materials are retained for **five years**. Retired members may need to request exemption annually. PAL applicants and holders must verify the separate IES 8 relevance conditions. [CPA Ontario CPD and Regulation 7-2 links](https://www.cpaontario.ca/members/regulations-guidance/continuing-professional-development)

CPA Ontario expressly permits personal tracking in any preferred tool. Its optional member web tracker lets users add/edit and print a summary. It neither auto-populates CPA Ontario courses nor links to the audit log. Selected members enter required audit information in the separate CPD Audit log in My Portal. The independent log is useful supporting preparation. Neither CSV import nor a formal external audit submission integration was verified. [CPA Ontario CPD, Tracking your CPD hours and FAQs](https://www.cpaontario.ca/members/regulations-guidance/continuing-professional-development)

## Proposed layout · 10 active fields

| Field | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Activity date, title, learning description | Three standard fields | Required for a traceable prepared log, with content relevant to role. |
| Duration | Standard duration | Count learning once overall. |
| Provider | Short text | Where applicable. |
| Learning activity type | Single-select | Distinguish formal courses, instruction, independent learning and others. |
| Verifiable time | Duration | Subset of qualifying total with evidence. |
| Verifiable professional ethics time | Duration | Subset of verifiable time. |
| Competency or role relevance | Short text | PAL claims need separate confirmation against their governing rules. |
| Evidence file | Standard attachment | Support verifiable entries and durable retention. |

Annual and rolling three-calendar-year views must share the same item classification. Four ethics hours means a threshold across the rolling period, including valid portions of courses. Avoid using a simple ethics checkbox to credit a complete activity. Support exemption and first-three-year boundary tests before presenting progress as definitive.

## Commercial assessment

CPA Ontario reports about **105,000 members**, an upper-bound audience with eligibility and exemptions unresolved. Its own tracker is direct competition and gives users a low-risk existing option. A local offline companion needs demonstrably better mobile evidence capture and ready-to-transcribe audit details. Medium build and maintenance effort for base membership, high if PAL or cross-province variants are bundled. The governing rule and guidance are high confidence. Willingness to pay and current portal screens have not been verified. [CPA Ontario membership snapshot](https://www.cpaontario.ca/about-cpa-ontario)
