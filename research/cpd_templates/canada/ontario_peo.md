# Canada · Ontario professional engineers and the PEAK transition

**Checked 20 November 2026.** Professional Engineers Ontario (PEO), Ontario licence holders under the Professional Engineers Act and Regulation 941. **Recommendation: defer.** Confirm the 2027 implementation and live reports after launch before offering a scheme-specific product.

## Rules distinguished by year

| Window | Official evidence |
|---|---|
| 2026 PEAK | Annual practice evaluation, professional practice module and CPD reporting for relevant practising members. Current personalised learning targets can reach 30 hours. The first two elements were due 31 January and the CPD report is due 31 December for the practising route. The year-end noncompliance notice does not extend the January deadline. [PEO key elements](https://www.peo.on.ca/licence-holders/mandatory-cpd/key-elements-cpd) |
| Announced 2027 | Last year of the practising/non-practising participation distinction. For the full programme, the regulator announces a learning plan, module, minimum 12 hours and learning activity report with outcomes. Single deadline 31 December 2027. Existing exemption boundaries still matter. |
| Planned 2028 | Full programme extended across practice statuses with stated exemptions, reflection added and deadline aligned to licence renewal. Treat as planned, subject to regulatory approval. |

[PEO mandatory CPD](https://www.peo.on.ca/licence-holders/mandatory-cpd) · [PEO 2027/2028 changes, 15 October 2026](https://www.peo.on.ca/latest-news/2027-2028-cpd-changes)

The current programme links admissible learning to professional practice risk and uses priority/supplementary constraints. The announced programme changes competency categories to technical, communication, project/financial management, team effectiveness, professional accountability, and social/economic/environmental sustainability. Do not apply that taxonomy retrospectively. The PEO portal remains the place to complete required steps. No independent log import or specific independent evidence-retention term was established. [PEO FAQs](https://www.peo.on.ca/knowledge-centre/frequently-asked-questions/peak-faqs) · [PEO transition](https://www.peo.on.ca/latest-news/2027-2028-cpd-changes)

## Research layout · 9 active fields

Date, title, activity detail, duration and attachment are the five standard fields. Add a controlled competency choice tied to the effective scheme version, practice-risk or learning-plan reference, outcome, and provider. Requiredness and named choices depend on the relevant year's live form. Keep practice declaration, module completion, formal reflection and learning plan at portfolio level. The report should aid portal entry with a year-specific copyable activity list.

PEO reported **91,456 P.Eng. licences as of 31 December 2025**, not equivalent to paying customers or currently practising participants. The transition could create a need for portable records, but a simplified official report with a small annual hours total may satisfy it. Wait for live portal/user validation. Build difficulty medium for a companion; maintenance temporarily high due to the transition. [PEO Annual Review 2025, p. 6](https://www.peo.on.ca/sites/default/files/2026-04/2025-annual-review.pdf)
