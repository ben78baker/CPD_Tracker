# Ontario nursing QA companion

**Jurisdiction** Canada · Ontario  
**Scope** College of Nurses of Ontario · General and Extended class  
**Recommendation** Suitable with a plan workflow; weak fit for an activity-only product.  
**Status** Research specification for review · 20 November 2026.

## Executive summary and official requirements

Annual self-assessment involves reflection, identifying learning needs and completing a learning plan. Annual renewal includes a QA declaration. Randomly selected nurses complete QA Assessment with knowledge modules and, for Part B, submit a plan and other materials for review. The cited programme is plan and competence based. No activity-hour quota or annual activity count is established by these sources. [C1 · Quality Assurance](https://www.cno.org/quality-assurance) · [C2 · QA Every Day](https://www.cno.org/quality-assurance/self-assessment)

## Evidence, official systems and reporting

Use the CNO learning-plan resources and preserve the applicant’s own reflections. A reviewer/peer coach is part of selected assessment, distinct from signing each log entry. The plan must include at least two learning goals. Nurses should keep it for two years and can choose a format such as the CNO template, their employer’s form or a notebook. Confirm the exact current plan template and submission path before release. [CNO Quality Assurance FAQs](https://www.cno.org/quality-assurance/faqs-quality-assurance-qa) Standalone support should allow a goal to span multiple activities and evaluate impact across them. [C1 · Quality Assurance](https://www.cno.org/quality-assurance) · [C2 · QA Every Day](https://www.cno.org/quality-assurance/self-assessment)

## Proposed individual record

**6 active fields** including the standard bindings listed below. M is mandatory for the indicated scheme or submission, C is conditional, R is recommended and O is optional. Where a regulator specifies an outcome without prescribing columns, the fields are product recommendations. M/C checks should surface when marking a record ready or preparing a scheme export, with incomplete historical data remaining editable. Show all rows for eligible records unless conditional, and omit blank optional values from PDF. Export rule exceptions appear below.

| Field | Type | Class | Purpose | Standard binding | Proposed validation | Reporting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Activity date | Date | R | Chronology of plan actions | date | completed date | period |
| Activity title | Text | R | Identify learning action | title | nonblank label | Detail only |
| Activity and impact | Long text | R | Record implementation and impact | details | anonymise narratives | Detail only |
| Learning goal reference | Text | R | Link action to annual learning plan | New | stable link preferred when supported | goal coverage |
| Supporting material | File | O | Retain material for plan evaluation | evidence | no identifiable patient data | Detail only |
| Time spent | Duration | O | Personal tracking | duration | zero allowed | personal only |

The specification derives from [C1 · Quality Assurance](https://www.cno.org/quality-assurance) and [C2 · QA Every Day](https://www.cno.org/quality-assurance/self-assessment). The types and validation wording are design recommendations. Choice, multi-choice and decimal inputs require separate feature approval.

## App fit and export boundary

The current app has one canonical activity date and duration, title, details, and general evidence. Standard bindings preserve those semantics. Existing PDF, PDF with evidence and CSV work from a profession and date selection and do not calculate this scheme’s eligibility or assemble its portfolio. In a scheme report, export applicable activity fields and evidence filenames/references by default. Make private reflections and original attachments selectable and keep private personal tracking out of formal totals where indicated. Review the [reporting gaps](../reporting_requirements.md) before making any product claims.

## Commercial potential, confidence and outstanding checks

Regional variant only. The goal-centred workflow needs more than a small record layout, and the official tools set expectations. Canadian provinces must be researched separately.

Core obligations above are supported by the linked official guidance. Field layouts are proposals. Commercial confidence is provisional without audience and purchase validation. Before approval, resolve the scope and workflow questions raised above and compare a representative draft and export with the latest regulator example or portal. Avoid assuming a rule from another profession where retention, signatures, categories or exceptions remain unverified.
