# New Zealand · Teacher Professional Growth Cycle

**Checked 20 November 2026.** Teaching Council of Aotearoa New Zealand. **Recommendation: reject an hours-based paid compliance template.** Consider lightweight reusable reflection fields only if teacher interviews establish unmet demand.

Annual Professional Growth Cycle participation and the professional leader's confirmation support the three-year practising certificate endorsement process. Element E expressly distinguishes naturally occurring evidence from any requirement to create evidence documents. The principal or leader confirms cycle participation and whether the teacher meets or is likely to meet the standards as appropriate to the certificate. This is primarily a professional dialogue and endorsement workflow. Do not impose a fixed hour target, personal evidence-pack duty or automatic certificate sign-off. Check provisional, relief-teacher and school-specific processes separately. [Teaching Council Element E](https://teachingcouncil.nz/index.htm/grow-my-practice/professional-growth/professional-growth-cycle/Element-E)

The 2026 standards inform re-certification from 2027. The Council explains that the three annual summaries used for endorsement can include both 2017 and 2026 standards through the transition. Preserve the historical standards version if offering tagging. The official My Rawa environment already offers reflective tools and official endorsement/renewal workflows. External submission or import was not established. ## Optional reflection layout · 8 active fields

Use the five standard fields (date, title, activity context, duration and optional attachment). Treat duration as personal information only. Add an optional goal reference, prompted reflection on changed practice, and optional standards mapping drawn from the setting's applicable version. The portfolio could export a private summary of learning conversations, subject to educator choice and privacy. Do not require an evidence upload or include identifying learner material unnecessarily. An official leader endorsement remains separate.

The Council distinguishes registered teachers from certificated teachers and breaks out full/provisional routes in its dashboard. No exact current target count or revenue evidence was extracted. Even if the eligible population is large, no obligation to build evidential documents and an official reflective environment make this a weak paid template. Medium design and maintenance effort for a valid standards-aligned portfolio. Low for generic field definitions already reusable elsewhere.
