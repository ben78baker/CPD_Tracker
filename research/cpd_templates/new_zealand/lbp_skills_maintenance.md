# New Zealand · Licensed Building Practitioner skills maintenance

**Checked 20 November 2026.** Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment (MBIE) Licensed Building Practitioners (LBP) scheme. **Recommendation: validate first.** Designed for LBP licence holders within their own class and period. It is not a general New Zealand construction or electrical CPD template.

## Requirements and workflow

LBPs must complete and record skills maintenance every two years before licence renewal. Carpentry, foundations, roofing, external plastering, and bricklaying/blocklaying require **12 elective hours**. Site and Design area 1 require **15**. Site and Design areas 2 or 3 require **18**. For multiple classes use the higher applicable elective-hour requirement. All categories require **two on-the-job learning activities** in total plus the prescribed class-specific articles and quizzes. Do not substitute extra elective hours for unfinished compulsory items. Annual sampling can lead to a request for records. [MBIE skills maintenance requirements and class table](https://www.lbp.govt.nz/for-lbps/skills-maintenance/)

The official site provides a RealMe-linked LBP portal and guides for recording and submitting activity. A third-party mobile record would support preparation; an import or automatic completion mark has not been verified. Confirm the audit evidence format, retention outside the cycle and how article-quiz completions appear in the portal before release. ## Proposed layout · 9 active fields

| Field | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Activity date, title, learning outcome | Three standard fields | Identify the learning and what changed in practice. |
| Duration | Standard duration | Eligible elective time where applicable. |
| Stream | Single-select | Elective, on-the-job learning, or compulsory article/quiz. |
| Relevant licence class | Multi-select | Portfolio reporting and competency relevance. |
| Provider, project or article identifier | Short text | Reference the course, application or Codewords item. |
| Quiz completion | Confirmation | For applicable compulsory items only. |
| Supporting evidence | Standard attachment | Keep proof available for audit and exclude client details as needed. |

Track each stream separately in a two-year progress summary. A photo-first worksite log is a plausible advantage. It competes with the official portal and an ordinary site diary, so test whether reducing capture time offsets re-entry. Medium implementation and maintenance burden, with a recurring need to refresh class and article mappings. Population and commercial take-up are unverified.
