# New Zealand registered and enrolled nurse development log

**Jurisdiction** New Zealand  
**Scope** Nursing Council · practising registered and enrolled nurses; separate practitioner pathway  
**Recommendation** Suitable with rolling summary and external audit workflow.  
**Status** Research specification for review · 20 November 2026.

## Executive summary and official requirements

At least 60 hours of development relevant to scope and 450 practice hours in the previous 36 months, plus compliance with competence standards. Eligible learning may include documented meetings with educational focus and journal discussion in a formal learning context. Annual practising certificate declaration is separate. Practice hours are a different ledger. [Z1 · Continuing competence](https://www.nursingcouncil.org.nz/Public/NCNZ/nursing-section/Continuing_Competence.aspx) · [Z2 · Recertification audit guidance for enrolled and registered nurses 2025](https://www.nursingcouncil.org.nz/common/Uploaded%20files/Public/Nursing/Recertification%20Audits/Recertification%20Audit%20guidance%20for%20enrolled%20and%20registered%20nurses%202025.pdf)

## Evidence, official systems and reporting

For audit, list date or period, topic, hours and the relevant pou (competence standard). The guide does not require professional-development certificates. Keep separate authentic competence examples for the audit. Audit guidance describes own authentic work, signed declaration and senior nurse or employer verification. MyNC is the submission channel. The guidance allows alternative audit templates covering all requirements. The downloadable development record form was inaccessible in this research tool, so exact field parity needs verification. No general retention period beyond the window was established. [Z1 · Continuing competence](https://www.nursingcouncil.org.nz/Public/NCNZ/nursing-section/Continuing_Competence.aspx) · [Z2 · Recertification audit guidance for enrolled and registered nurses 2025](https://www.nursingcouncil.org.nz/common/Uploaded%20files/Public/Nursing/Recertification%20Audits/Recertification%20Audit%20guidance%20for%20enrolled%20and%20registered%20nurses%202025.pdf)

## Proposed individual record

**7 active fields** including the standard bindings listed below. M is mandatory for the indicated scheme or submission, C is conditional, R is recommended and O is optional. Where a regulator specifies an outcome without prescribing columns, the fields are product recommendations. M/C checks should surface when marking a record ready or preparing a scheme export, with incomplete historical data remaining editable. Show all rows for eligible records unless conditional, and omit blank optional values from PDF. Export rule exceptions appear below.

## App fit and export boundary

The current app has one canonical activity date and duration, title, details, and general evidence. Standard bindings preserve those semantics. Existing PDF, PDF with evidence and CSV work from a profession and date selection and do not calculate this scheme’s eligibility or assemble its portfolio. In a scheme report, export applicable activity fields and evidence filenames/references by default. Make private reflections and original attachments selectable and keep private personal tracking out of formal totals where indicated. Review the [reporting gaps](../reporting_requirements.md) before making any product claims.

## 20 November follow-up

The Council’s current competence guidance still gives the 60-hour three-year development minimum. New registered nurse standards apply generally from 1 January 2026, so use version-aware pou mappings. The Council reported 85,462 annual practising certificate holders at 31 March 2025, including nurse practitioners, and estimates about 13% were not actively practising in-country. This is a dated upper-bound proxy rather than a customer forecast.
