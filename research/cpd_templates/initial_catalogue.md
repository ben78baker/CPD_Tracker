# Recommended initial catalogue

See the [current ranked ten](#ranked-first-ten--20-november-2026) for the expanded programme recommendation.

## Earlier shortlist · 19 November 2026

1. **NMC Revalidation CPD Log.** Research-backed participatory hours, Code mapping and evidence. Require the separate participatory subtotal and confirm the official example. Market the activity log separately from the full revalidation portfolio. [Specification](uk/nmc.md)
2. **GDC Enhanced CPD Log.** One layout across eligible dental titles with role-specific scheme settings. Require precise minutes, verifiable evidence status, development outcomes and both time windows. Avoid temporary registration claims pending verification. [Specification](uk/gdc.md)
3. **HCPC Personal CPD Log.** One shared scheme layout initially, with outcomes and selective evidence rather than profession-name clones. Provide an audit preparation export only after testing it against the official process. [Specification](uk/hcpc.md)
4. **ACCA Standard Unit Route.** Strong differentiation beyond healthcare. Require verifiable and non-verifiable summaries, evidence and annual carry-forward clarity. [Specification](uk/acca.md)

**Next candidates** IET CPD and SRA continuing competence. Social Work England merits a draft workflow pilot once long-text transfer and anonymity are tested. Validate RCVS, RICS, GPhC and IOSH before finalising the next ranking. Early source access gaps may hide stronger commercial opportunities.

## International sequencing

| Market | First candidate | Why separate |
|---|---|---|
| Ireland | [CORU physiotherapy](ireland/coru_physiotherapy.md) | Credits, twelve-month audit and portal copying differ from HCPC. Confirm current board guide. |
| Australia | [CPA Australia](australia/cpa_australia.md) | Triennium and verified ethics require settings distinct from ACCA and ICAEW. NMBA nursing remains an access-blocked lead. |
| New Zealand | [Registered and enrolled nursing](new_zealand/nursing.md) | 60 development hours, rolling window, separate practice hours and competency verification differ from NMC. |
| Canada | [Ontario nursing QA](canada/ontario_nursing.md) | Goal-centred learning plan and assessment make it a different product from a timed CPD log. |
| United States | [California RN continuing education](usa/california_rn.md) | Recognised providers, contact hours, exceptions and retention require state-specific design. |

Cross-market sharing should occur only where a named scheme explicitly spans the same route and obligations, such as the ACCA standard route where no local overlay applies. Reuse components instead of implying shared compliance. Approval requires verified source versions, a signed-off field and reporting specification, sample exports tested against the actual workflow, and explicit implementation authorisation.

All layouts count standard fields and remain under 20. M indicates a per-record source requirement or an important required condition, R recommended and O optional. For portable families, share field and reporting components. One GDC template can serve roles with different profiles, and HCPC does not need occupation clones of the same requirements. Separate NMC, New Zealand, Australian and California nursing because time, evidence and renewal semantics differ substantially. Strong next international pilots include NSW Proficient Teacher PD, CPA Ontario and New Zealand nursing. See the [source and coverage review](international/international_sources.md). Defer RCVS, RICS and IOSH unless testing shows material benefit alongside their required systems. Do not market an independent log as the regulator’s tool or as endorsed, compliant or submission-ready without evidence and clearance.
