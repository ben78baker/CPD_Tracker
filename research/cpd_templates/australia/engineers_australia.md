# Australia · Engineers Australia Chartered and NER

**Checked 20 November 2026.** Applies to Chartered members of Engineers Australia and National Engineering Register (NER) registrants, including non-members. **Recommendation: validate first as a specialist companion.** This is a credential policy. Queensland RPEQ statutory registration and other state schemes remain separate.

## Requirements and boundaries

The policy requires **150 hours in the previous three years**, including at least **50 hours per area of engineering practice**, **10 hours risk management** and **15 hours business and management skills**. An overlapping technical activity may be credited to multiple practice areas; the 150-hour total cannot be computed by summing all area subtotals. Engineering academics and teachers have a 40-hour industry involvement component. The policy extends the CPD lookback to five years for qualifying part-time employment or approved breaks and defines those conditions. The usual **audit and practice review cadence is five years**, separate from the default three-year learning window. [Engineers Australia CPD policy v2.1, pp. 1–3, 24 January 2025](https://www.engineersaustralia.org.au/sites/default/files/2025-02/continuing-professional-development-policy-v2-1.pdf)

The public review form requests an experience statement focused on areas and responsibilities and documented activities. Store portable evidence and editable narrative alongside activity records, with verification of live submission formats before release. A separate evidence-retention rule beyond the review expectations was not established in this pass. The policy lists **11 December 2026** as the next review date. Queensland published a separate 2026 update, corroborating why a similar headline threshold does not warrant one generic Australian compliance product. ## Proposed layout · 10 active fields

| Field | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Activity date, title and learning detail | Three standard fields | Preserve activity record and context. |
| Eligible duration | Standard duration | One contribution to overall total. |
| Practice areas | Multi-select | Preserve area-specific mappings and valid overlaps. |
| Development domain | Controlled choice | Distinguish technical, risk and management categories. |
| Activity type | Controlled choice | Store audit classification independently of domain. |
| Provider or project context | Short text | Document participation without client-sensitive data. |
| Industry participation | Confirmation | Conditional for academics or teachers. |
| Evidence | Standard attachment | Retain review material. |

A grouped hours summary needs explicit area-credit logic, configurable review windows and support for activity-category caps if confirmed by the published Types and Conditions guide. Add a practice statement at report level. The cost of policy-specific calculation and maintaining overlapping state rules makes a flat, generic engineering layout a weak paid offer. Validate the credential-holder segment and current Engineers Australia upload/template usability before pricing.
