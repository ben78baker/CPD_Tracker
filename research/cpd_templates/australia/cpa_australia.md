# CPA Australia member CPD

**Scope** Members in scope of CPA Australia CPD by-laws. Separate regulated licence requirements and exemptions need review.  
**Recommendation** Suitable with annual, triennial and ethics reporting.  
**Status** Research specification for review · 19 November 2026.

## Requirements and evidence

The ordinary scheme specifies at least 20 hours per year and 120 hours per triennium. The published ethics minimum is two verifiable hours per year and ten per triennium. Determine the member’s triennium from the official CPD Diary and joining rules. Verify ethics completion with a certificate or attendance confirmation. Registration confirmation and course slides alone are inadequate proof of ethics participation. The diary automatically records provider-facilitated CPA Australia learning. Other activities may have caps, including structured reading and mentoring. Ethics rules include transition cases by triennium end year. Confirm those rules, recognised employers and by-law exemptions before using automated totals for formal reporting. [CPA Australia requirements](https://www.cpaaustralia.com.au/professional-development/continuing-professional-development)

## Proposed record

**Eight active fields**, including standard bindings. M is mandatory for the targeted requirement, C is conditional and R is recommended. Export all applicable populated values with evidence references. Offer evidence originals separately and distinguish logged hours from eligible ethics totals.

| Field | Type | Class | Purpose | Standard binding | Proposed validation | Reporting |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Completion date | Date | R | Select correct year and triennium | Date | No future completion | Period |
| Activity title | Text | R | Identify completed learning | Title | Nonblank | Detail |
| Learning and relevance | Long text | R | Demonstrate professional relevance | Details | Explain role relationship | Detail |
| CPD time | Duration | M | Track eligible hours | Duration | Preserve minutes | Year and triennium |
| Learning category | Choice | R | Identify nature of CPD | New | Check against official eligible categories | Category grouping |
| Ethics hours | Duration | C | Track qualifying ethics subset | New | Between zero and total, evidence required if claimed | Ethics totals |
| Provider | Text | R | Identify source of learning | New | Preserve supplied name | Detail |
| Evidence | File | C | Retain verifiable proof | Evidence | Certificate, attendance confirmation or traceable reference | Evidence list |

Field labels and form rules are proposed mappings, informed by the official requirements and recording guidance. [Recording CPD hours](https://www.cpaaustralia.com.au/professional-development/continuing-professional-development/recording-your-cpd)

## Reporting, app limitations and opportunity

Use a year and triennium summary, ethics eligibility checks and a reviewable evidence list. The current tracker’s single duration total cannot represent both clocks or the verified ethics subset. CPA Australia already provides an online diary. Confirm evidence retention, formal review transfer, and any declaration or sign-off rules before claiming audit readiness. The layout could reuse accounting field components, but should be presented separately from ACCA and ICAEW. Demand and reachable member counts remain unverified. Official hours and ethics rules have higher confidence than product fit.
