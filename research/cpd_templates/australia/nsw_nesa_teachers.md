# Australia · NSW NESA teacher professional development log

**Checked 20 November 2026.** NSW Education Standards Authority (NESA), Proficient Teacher maintenance. **Recommendation: validate first as an offline companion.** High confidence in the activity requirements and independent log route. Additional Highly Accomplished and Lead Teacher assessments sit outside this scope.

## Requirements and accepted workflow

Teachers maintaining Proficient accreditation complete **100 hours** of professional development (PD) related to the Australian Professional Standards for Teachers in their assigned maintenance period. Periods typically run five years for full-time work or seven for part-time/casual work. Use the actual eTAMS dates. Effective 16 August 2024, the former Accredited and Elective category split was removed. The current priority areas inform professional learning. They do not impose mandatory category allocations. Routine classroom duties may be ineligible even when they take time. [NSW Government change notice, 22 August 2024](https://www.nsw.gov.au/media-releases/more-valuable-professional-learning-for-teachers-to-deliver-greater-impact-classroom) · [NESA Manual section A4](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/teacher-accreditation/resources/nsw-ta-manual/a4) · [NESA PD requirements](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/teacher-accreditation/maintain-accreditation/professional-development-requirements)

The log is retained for **12 months after maintenance submission**. Selected audit respondents can email a personal log or confirm that the official eTAMS record is complete. NESA describes eTAMS as the fastest audit route and it totals the hours. The regulator supplies a personal log PDF. Attendance certificates and evaluations are not required for the log audit. That makes structured, legible field export more valuable than evidence-heavy PDF packaging in this particular scheme. [NESA PD requirements, Recording your PD and PD log audit](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/teacher-accreditation/maintain-accreditation/professional-development-requirements) · [NESA PD log, August 2024](https://www.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-08/nesa-professional-development-log-august-2024.pdf)

| Field | Type | Status |

| Date | Standard date | Required. |

| Activity detail | Standard long text | Optional personal notes or learning impact. |

Report the assigned cycle, required columns and a 100-hour progress numerator. Provide a simple table readable by NESA staff. Maintenance decision, employer aspects and eTAMS submission remain separate. No import or automated eligibility confirmed.

## Opportunity and limits

This is a cleaner third-party log opportunity than schemes that force per-activity portal entry. A free PDF and eTAMS already meet the basic job. Validate demand with teachers who log across employers or frequently work offline. Medium implementation burden for controlled Standards and cycle totals. Medium maintenance given the 2024 category changes and the need to cross-check official choices. NESA published **177,013 accredited teachers** and **19,580 completed Proficient maintenance events** in 2024–25. The first is a broad population and the second a period count. Eligible app users and willingness to pay remain unverified. [NESA Annual Report 2024–25, p. 36](https://www.parliament.nsw.gov.au/tp/files/192015/NESA%20Annual%20Report%202024%E2%80%9325.pdf) Other states require distinct research.
