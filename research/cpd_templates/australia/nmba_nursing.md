# Australia · NMBA nursing and midwifery CPD

**Checked 20 November 2026.** National registration standard under the National Law as applied in Australian states and territories. **Recommendation: validate first, high-priority international candidate.** High confidence in the 2016 framework and 2024 factsheet. A 2026 consultation means the next effective standard and dates require a release check.

## Rules, cycle and evidence

The Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia (NMBA) explains a standard minimum of **20 hours per registration type annually**. Non-practising registrants are excluded. Nurse practitioners and particular endorsed roles have extra subject-specific requirements. Someone registered for both nursing and midwifery should plan against each requirement. The regulator explicitly allows relevant activity to count towards both, so a single global total is insufficient. Use actual relevant learning and seek confirmation before converting points from another organisation. Mandatory workplace education counts only where relevant to practice and with new learning. [NMBA revised standard notice](https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/News/2016-02-01-revised-midwifery-standards) · [NMBA factsheet, pp. 1–4](https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?chksum=glBIRNUIK27TneX%2BdJRYzg%3D%3D&dbid=AP&record=WD16%2F19495)

Registration renewal includes a CPD declaration. If audited, provide the evidence requested and the CPD plan for **1 June–31 May**. The factsheet recommends five years of evidence retention, including references for self-directed learning. Its Appendix 1 is a usable template. None of the inspected sources establishes automated third-party import or regulator endorsement of a standalone product. The regulator's example and employer portfolios are alternatives. [NMBA factsheet, pp. 2–6](https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/documents/default.aspx?chksum=glBIRNUIK27TneX%2BdJRYzg%3D%3D&dbid=AP&record=WD16%2F19495)

## Proposed layout · 11 active fields

| Field | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Activity date | Standard date | Include in the correct year. |
| Activity title | Standard short text | Identify activity or topic. |
| Topic and learning outcome | Standard long text | Summarise content and result. |
| CPD duration | Standard duration | Actual qualifying hours and minutes. |
| Source or provider | Short text | Include reference for self-directed reading when applicable. |
| Learning need | Long text | Map to the portfolio need. |
| Planned action | Short text | Link or summarise development plan. |
| Activity type | Single-select | Distinguish self-directed and other types without inventing quotas. |
| Application to practice | Long text | Reflection and context relevance. |
| Registration or endorsement relevance | Multi-select | Conditional. Support separate views of the same activity without double-counting the overall activity. |
| Supporting evidence | Standard attachment | Certificate, reading reference or other documentation. |

The rules require a CPD record and relevant evidence. The proposed labels and validation reflect the factsheet example and do not represent separately mandated UI fields. Keep the learning plan and renewal statement at portfolio level. ## Commercial and technical assessment

Ahpra reported **more than 510,000 nurses and midwives successfully renewing during the 2024/25 period**. This is a historical count of renewals, not prospective customers. Annual audit preparation creates a credible mobile capture opportunity. Test the added value against employer systems, the official record example and the Ahpra renewal portal. Initial effort is medium for a single-registration audit companion, high if multi-registration and endorsement allocation are included. Maintenance is high until the 2026 national review outcome is known. The consultation closed on 17 July 2026 and the current source set did not establish a final replacement standard. Keep the factsheet's May 2027 review date distinct from the cross-board policy consultation.
