## Rules and workflow

The **2026 calendar-year cycle** ordinarily requires **25 hours**, including at least **five** professional development and solicitor wellbeing hours and **three** client care and professional standards hours. For sole practitioners and designated compliance or AML partners, one hour of the three must cover accounting/AML. Senior practitioner and modification pathways have different totals. The ordinary minimum includes at least **five hours or 20%** in qualifying group study. Daily learning is capped at **seven hours**. Eligible eLearning and writing have category caps, with modifications. Group study has physical attendance and minimum-session conditions. [Law Society 2026 rules and options](https://www.lawsociety.ie/cpd-compliance/current-cycle-information/) · [2024–26 scheme booklet](https://www.lawsociety.ie/globalassets/documents/cpd-scheme/2026/2024-26-cpd-scheme-booklet.pdf)

The official editable record card records date, course, provider, CPD hours and category by format. Retain the card with verifying proofs and provide it only when requested. The official 2026 sources reviewed here did not verify a general numeric retention term. An older booklet referenced six years and included earlier schemes, so confirm current applicability rather than hard code it. The law society sets written proof expectations for providers. Independent capture may feed the official record card. No bulk upload or replacement-card approval verified.
