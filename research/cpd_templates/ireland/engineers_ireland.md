# Engineers Ireland member CPD

**Scope** Engineers Ireland members within policy. Official overview excludes students and retirees from the ordinary requirement. Accredited Employer and exemption provisions have different workflows.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high on ordinary rule and declaration tools.  
**Recommendation** Validate later as a separate scheme configuration. Reuse common engineering fields without reusing UK thresholds.

## Rules and workflow

Members ordinarily undertake and record **35 hours each year**. Varied work, mentoring, technical study and formal courses can be eligible. The My CPD guide describes activity duration from **30 minutes to 35 hours**, splitting larger activities into components, and recommends limiting self-directed learning to **14 hours**. It provides categories, provider fields, a route for multi-date activities and completed education. Treat the 14-hour advice as guidance until its enforceable form is confirmed. Record and declare in My CPD, which already supports export and member workflows. An eligible Accredited Employer route lets a member upload employer documentation for the declaration. This does not establish general bulk import for an independent app. [Engineers Ireland Record My CPD](https://www.engineersireland.ie/Professionals/CPD-Careers/Record-My-CPD) · [CPD policy](https://www.engineersireland.ie/Professionals/CPD-Careers/Record-My-CPD/CPD-policy) · [My CPD guide](https://www.engineersireland.ie/Professionals/CPD-Careers/Record-My-CPD/My-CPD-guide)

The provider tool’s declaration, employment route and evidence export are core competitors. Confirm current year-close deadline and date basis, specific retiree status, evidence retention, monitoring and report fields directly before marketing. A 31 January deadline appearing in an older notice for the 2022 year is not evidence of the upcoming date. [Engineers Ireland historical notice](https://www.engineersireland.ie/Engineers-Journal/News/fao-members-dont-miss-january-31-deadline-for-declaring-your-cpd-activities)
