# Ireland nursing and midwifery professional competence watch

**Status** Partial verification only · 20 November 2026.  
**Recommendation** Defer a scheme template until the final scheme and implementation status are directly published and reviewed.

The Nursing and Midwifery Board of Ireland (NMBI) states that it is revising its Professional Competence Scheme after a pilot and discourages adopting the existing pilot model. The regulator web page says the finalised scheme is expected in November 2026. This is a published expectation, not confirmation that the final rules are live. The current Code expects registrants to maintain professional competence and keep their knowledge and skills current, including those retaining registration while retired. Part 11 of the Nurses and Midwives Act 2011 is described on the page as not yet commenced. Verify the final scheme, commencement and transition before committing dates, credits, evidence format or portal assumptions. Do not substitute the UK NMC scheme. [NMBI Professional Competence Scheme](https://www.nmbi.ie/Standards-Guidance/Professional-Competence-Scheme)
