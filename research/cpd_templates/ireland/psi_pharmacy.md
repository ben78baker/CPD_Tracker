# Ireland pharmacist CPD and IIOP ePortfolio

**Scope** Pharmacists registered with Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland (PSI). Excludes pharmaceutical assistants and separate UK regulators.  
**Verification** 20 November 2026 · high on official tool and annual-review transition.  
**Recommendation** Defer a paid template unless a practitioner study establishes value from a personal draft companion.

## Rules and recent change

The Pharmacy Act and PSI CPD rules require registrants to develop and maintain competence and keep their CPD via **IIOP ePortfolio**. Relevant formal learning and informal practice-based learning can count, with self-reflection about needs and improved practice. No independent tool is provided for official ePortfolio review. Annual continued registration includes professional declarations, and the 2025 amendments link review engagement to continuation. [PSI ePortfolio Review](https://www.psi.ie/education-and-training/continuing-professional-development-cpd/eportfolio-review-process) · [PSI FAQ](https://www.psi.ie/education-and-training/continuing-professional-development-cpd/frequently-asked-questions-about) · [IIOP CPD overview](https://iiop.ie/content/new-to-practice/legislative-regulatory-information/continuing-professional-development)

The previous longer review cycle has moved to annual review. The 2026/27 cohort includes eligible pharmacists on the PSI register before the 2026 cutoff, with submission in **January 2027** unless exempt. Those first registered during 2026 generally join the following review period. The IIOP workflow accepts an extract written in the official portfolio. Verify boundary language with PSI for a registrant first registered on 1 January 2026, exact dates, the active review standard, cycle count and retention before designing calculations. No general hours target established. [PSI FAQ, eligibility and exemption](https://www.psi.ie/education-and-training/continuing-professional-development-cpd/frequently-asked-questions-about)

## Draft layout · 8 active fields

| Field | Type and use |
|---|---|
| Activity date | Standard date; personal chronology. |
| Activity title | Standard short text. |
| Activity context | Standard details, anonymised. |
| Time spent | Standard duration; optional internal metric. |
| Identified learning need | Long text, personal prompt. |
| Planned learning | Long text, context for follow-up. |
| Reflection and impact | Long text; how learning was used and effect on practice. |
| Supporting evidence | Standard file; keep private or selectively export. |

This is an optional drafting map, not a clone of the evolving IIOP criteria. The app’s authorised use is as a personal aid. Data captured independently still needs to go into IIOP. The mandatory portal, quick policy changes and personal confidentiality materially reduce the attractiveness of a £3.99 offering.
